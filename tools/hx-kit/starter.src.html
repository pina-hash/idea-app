Learn

Zero your caliper

  1. Get a digital caliper and turn it on.
  2. Wipe both jaws with a clean rag.
  3. Close the jaws fully.
  4. Press ZERO.

0.000 with the jaws closed. Open and close once: it comes back to 0.000.

Measure

Measure your part

  1. Pick one feature of the part you made in your last build. Its drawing gives the designed value and the tolerance.
  2. A width: the big outside jaws. A hole: the small inside jaws.
  3. Close the jaws square to the faces, with light thumb pressure.
  4. Read it, open the jaws, lift off. Do it three times.

Three readings within 0.002 in of each other.

That is not what I see
The readings differ by more than 0.002The jaws are tilted. Square them to the faces and go lighter.
One reading is much biggerA burr is in the way. Measure away from the edge.
No tolerance on the drawingLook in the title block: the general tolerance covers every size without its own.

Photograph it

  1. Close the jaws on the same feature again.
  2. Photograph the part with the display in the shot.
  3. Paste the photo into the box, or press Choose photo.
  4. Turn the caliper off and put it back where you got it.
