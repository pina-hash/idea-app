Leaderboard Stats
Lifetime Earned
Awarded coins minus fines. Determines your leaderboard rank. Spending coins does not affect this number, and neither does a refund or any other balance correction.
Coin Balance
Your current economic standing. Awarded minus fines minus spent, plus any adjustments. Reflects how many coins you effectively own right now.
Net Awards
Total coins awarded to you across all time before any deductions.
Net Fines
Total coins fined from you across all time.
Net Spent
Total coins spent on purchases across all time.
Adjustments
Refunds and other corrections to your record, added up with their own signs. They move your balance but are neither earnings nor spending, so they never change your Lifetime Earned rank. Shown only if you have any.
