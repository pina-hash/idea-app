Unit 2 Documentation Check

IDEA209H · 25 points · Engineering Documentation

There is nothing to upload here.

Your notebook is already uploaded on the Portfolio Capture, and this is graded from those same photos. Upload each page once, there.

What is graded here

This is the one place your notebook is graded on how you kept it. Four things, and they are the four printed in your syllabus.

  1. An entry for every session.7 pts
    Every station day, the planning work, and the measuring.
  2. Written at the machine, not copied out later.6 pts
    Numbers as you took them. Crossed-out readings stay. A page that looks too tidy usually means it was written up afterward.
  3. Dated, and written so it can be read.6 pts
    Every entry carries its date.
  4. A notebook someone else could work from.6 pts
    Another student could read it and repeat the unit without asking you anything.

Nothing is scored twice

Scored here

  • Whether every session has an entry
  • Whether it was written at the machine
  • Whether it is dated and readable as written
  • Whether someone else could work from it

Scored on the Capture instead

  • What your station records actually say
  • Whether your drawings could be built from
  • Whether your photos are readable

A thin station record costs you on the Capture, not here. An undated page costs you here, not there.

A messy page with real numbers on it beats a clean page copied out the night before.

If a page is missing or was written late, say so on the page itself. Marking it late is worth more than leaving a gap.
