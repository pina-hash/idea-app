Shop Trophy: Portfolio Capture

IDEA209H · 75 points · three weeks of work, captured once

Read this firstnot graded

This is the only place your notebook gets uploaded. The Documentation Check grades these same photos. You do not upload anything there.

One photo per page. Flat, straight on, in good light. If a page is not readable it cannot be scored. More pages than slots? Photograph two facing pages in one shot.

Missing a page? Write it now from memory and mark it as written late. A late honest record beats a blank.

What each part of this is graded onnot graded
  • Your notebook pages: what your five station records say. The machine and the part, the settings you actually used, the tool or workholding you chose, and what happened.
  • Your drawings: whether someone who has never seen the model could make the part from your drawing.
  • The trophy: whether it went together, and what you noticed when it did or did not.
  • Not graded here: dating, handwriting and whether every session has an entry. Those are the Documentation Check.

The tool you chose goes in the record. You explain why in your presentation, not here.

Your five drawings

One per part. The standard: someone who has never seen the model could make the part from your drawing. Every dimension needed to manufacture it, every feature. The topper is your own sketch with its two constraints marked: 2.5 inches maximum across, and the 1/4 inch hole at the center.

Changed something between drawing the part and making it? Mark it on the drawing. A drawing that matches the part you actually made beats a clean one that does not.
