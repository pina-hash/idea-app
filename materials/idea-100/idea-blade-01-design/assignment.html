IDEA-Blade 01 - Design

Your team designs one Blade. You sketch it together on paper, model your part in SolidWorks, hand in the file that gets made, and check the whole Blade against the rules.

Four classes; the plan below says what your role does in each one. You write three sentences in the whole thing, one at a time. Everything else is a pick, a number, a photo or a file.

      What your sketch has to fit

      • Size: 5.00 in across at most. 3.00 in tall at most, tip (The point at the very bottom that the Blade spins on. It is a bolt from the shop.) to the top of the collar. At least 0.50 in of bare hex (The hex sticking up above the collar with nothing on it. The launcher grips this part.) on top.
      • Weight: 1.5 lb (680 g) at most, ready to fight.
      • One new blade per team. It is laser-cut from 0.100 in steel, and steel is short, so it is the only steel part made new. Add as many leftover blades (Steel blades cut for earlier Blades. The pile is free to use, and every one fits any hex.) as you like: every one has a 0.50 in hex bore (The six-sided hole in the middle of a blade. It slides onto the hex and turns with it.) and slides onto any hex.
      • Everything else is printed, any shape you can design: body, fins, weight ring, tip.
      • It has to fight. An edge that hits must grip and push, not let the other Blade slide past.

        How Armory works

        • Open is look only. Check out and open to change a file. One editor at a time.
        • Done editing: Ctrl+S, close with the inner X, then Check in.
        • Before you leave: check in everything. A teammate may need it while you are out.
        • Save only into your team's folder. Never Desktop or Downloads: your team cannot see those.
        • Never check out a template, the hex core (The steel hex shaft through the middle of the stack. It comes from the shop.), the collar or the bolt. Open them only.
        • One file per part. Save over it. Armory keeps every old version, so no "v2", "use this" or "best one" copies.
        • Nothing is lost. Every save is kept as a version.
