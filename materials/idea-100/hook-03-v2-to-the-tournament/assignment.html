Done

Pick where it broke in 1, and the fix for that spot shows here.

Thicken the ring

  • Edit the sketch and make the outer circle of the top ring bigger.
  • The hole stays 1.000 in or more. Only the ring around it grows.

The pull runs through the ring on both sides of the hole. More ring, less stress in it.

Round the inside corner

  • Find the sharp inside corner where it broke: under the ring, or where the shank turns into the bowl.
  • Make that Fillet bigger, or add one if there is none.

A sharp inside corner piles the stress up in one spot, and cracks start there. A bigger round spreads it out.

Widen the shank

  • Edit the sketch and move the shank’s outside line out.
  • Keep the opening 0.500 in or more.

The shank is pulled straight. Its stress is the load divided by its area, so more width, less stress.

Make the bowl deeper

  • Edit the sketch and make the bowl’s outer circle bigger, moving it down.
  • The inside circle stays 1.000 in or more, and the opening stays 0.500 in or more.

The bowl bends, and in bending the depth counts twice. A tip that pulled open is the bowl bending too far.

Give the joint more grip

  • Make the keys bigger, or add another one, so more material holds the pieces together.
  • Round the inside corners of each key.
  • It still has to open and close by hand in 10 seconds.

A joint is a place where the pull has to pass through less material than anywhere else. Give it more.

Pay for it in grams

  • Under 50.0 g with the change? You are done.
  • Over? Take material from a place your test showed was fine: no crack, no white marks.

Material is never free. A gram added at the break is worth more than a gram anywhere else.

Two small faces: one where the dowel touches the top loop, one where the rope sits in the bowl. The study needs both.

Top loop: where the dowel touches

  1. Click Front Plane (the plane you drew the hook on), then Sketch tab, Sketch.
  2. Draw a circle about the size of the dowel, about halfway across the inside of the top loop. Eyeball it: no dimensions.
  3. Features tab, the little arrow under Curves, Split Line.
  4. Top box: your circle sketch. Pick Projection. Click in the second box, then click the inside round face of the top loop (every face there, if it is made of several). Green check.

Bowl: where the rope sits

  1. Front Plane, Sketch again. A smaller circle where the rope rests, about halfway into the inside curve of the bowl.
  2. Curves, Split Line, the inside round face of the bowl, green check.

You should seeA small face of its own in the top loop, and another in the bowl, each with a line around it. Screenshot both.

That’s not what I see 7 fixes
  • Can’t find Split LineSearch box at the top right: set it to Commands, type split line. Click the little i and SolidWorks points at the button.
  • The circle is a straight lineYou sketched on the wrong plane. Exit the sketch, delete it, start again on Front Plane.
  • Split Line will not finishThe circle has to cross the face. Edit the sketch and drag the circle so it overlaps the inside curve.
  • The whole loop changed, no small faceThe circle swallowed the whole loop. Make it smaller, about the dowel’s size.
  • Picked an edge instead of the faceRight-click in the box, Clear Selections, click the middle of the round face.
  • Not sure you are in a sketchThe bar at the bottom of the screen says Editing Sketch.
  • Can’t see inside the loopHold the middle mouse button and drag to spin. Scroll to zoom. F fits the hook on screen.
