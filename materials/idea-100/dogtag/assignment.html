1Open the template in SolidWorksSolidWorks
  • Under Files on this assignment, download the template and open it.
  • File, Save As, Desktop. Name it dogtag-yourname.
  • Bottom right corner says IPS.
Every click
  1. On this assignment page, find Files. Click the SolidWorks template to download it.
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the template. SolidWorks (the red cube icon) opens it. The first time can take a minute.
  3. SolidWorks did not open it? Open SolidWorks yourself, then click File, Open, Downloads, pick the template, and click Open.
  4. Click File in the top left, then Save As. Click Desktop in the list on the left.
  5. In File name, type dogtag- and your name, like dogtag-maria-lopez. Click Save. From now on you work in this copy.
  6. Look at the bottom right corner of SolidWorks. It should say IPS. If it says MMGS or Custom, click it and pick IPS (inch, pound, second).

You got it if the top of SolidWorks says dogtag-yourname, the bottom right says IPS, and you can see the 2 × 2 box.

A pop-up or tip shows up
Click OK or close it. Nothing in it matters for this.
You cannot see the box
Press F to zoom to fit, or scroll to zoom out.
You cannot find the template
It is under Files on this page, not inside these steps. Still missing? Raise your hand.
2Draw your tag inside the boxSolidWorks
  • Edit the sketch that holds the box. Draw with Line, Arc and Circle, all inside the box.
  • One closed outline, plus at least one circle for the chain. More holes and cut-outs are fine.
Every click
  1. In the design tree on the left, click the sketch that holds the box, then click Edit Sketch (the pencil). The box is made of dashed construction lines: it is your limit, and it never gets cut.
  2. On the Sketch tab, click Line. Click to start a line, click to end it. For a classic tag, draw one line across the top and one across the bottom.
  3. Press Esc to drop the tool. Click the Arc dropdown and pick 3 Point Arc. Click the end of the top line, click the end of the bottom line, then move the mouse out and click to set the curve. Do both sides.
  4. Click Circle. Click inside the tag near an edge for the center, move out a little, and click. That is your chain hole. Keep metal all the way around it.
  5. Want more holes or cut-outs? Draw them the same way. Any closed shape inside the outline gets cut out. Keep them away from the edge.
  6. Want an exact size? Click Smart Dimension, click the circle, click to place the number, type 0.2, press Enter.
  7. Press Esc a few times when you are done, so your mouse is just a pointer again.

You got it if the outline is one closed shape with no gaps, everything sits inside the box, and there is at least one hole.

A line keeps drawing from the last one
Press Esc once, then click where the next line starts. Double-clicking also ends a line.
Clicking a line draws instead of selecting
A tool is still on. Press Esc a few times.
Two ends do not meet
Drag one end point onto the other until it snaps. A gap stops the extrude in step 3.
The sketch is gray and will not let you draw
You left the sketch. Click it in the design tree, then Edit Sketch.
You made a mess
Ctrl + Z undoes the last thing. Press it as many times as you need.
3Extrude it, then save a DXFSolidWorks
  • Features, Extruded Boss/Base, any thickness, green check. Ctrl + S.
  • Click the top face. File, Save As, type DXF, Save. Pick the face, green check, Save.
Every click
  1. Click the Features tab at the top, then Extruded Boss/Base.
  2. Any thickness works. Only the flat outline goes to the laser. Click the green check.
  3. Press Ctrl + S to save your part.
  4. Click the top face of your tag once, so it lights up. Just the flat top, one click.
  5. Click File, Save As. Click Desktop. Keep the same name.
  6. Open the Save as type list and pick Dxf (*.dxf). Click Save.
  7. A DXF/DWG Output panel opens on the left. Under Export, pick Faces/Loops/Edges. Click in the selection box, then click the top face once, so the box lists one face.
  8. Click the green check. A flat preview of your tag opens. Click Save.

You got it if your Desktop has two files named dogtag-yourname: the SolidWorks part and the DXF.

The extrude will not go, or warns about the sketch
There is a gap in the outline or lines crossing. Go back into the sketch and close it. Or open Selected Contours in the extrude panel and click the area of the tag between the outline and the holes.
The chain hole came out filled in
In Selected Contours you clicked the hole too. Click only the tag area around it.
Save as type has no DXF
You are still in the sketch. Click Exit Sketch in the top right corner, then try again.
The preview is empty
No face was picked. Go back to the Output panel and click the top face once.
4Open xTool Studio, bring in your DXFxTool Studio
  • The green square with the black X. New Project, Device Library, MetalFab CNC Cutter.
  • The + (Import) on the left, Desktop, your DXF.
Every click
  1. On the taskbar, click the green square with a black X. The one with four white squares is a different program.
  2. Not on the taskbar? Press key, type xtool, and press Enter. It can take 20 seconds to open.
  3. A welcome screen or pop-up? Click Next, then Get started. Any button that moves you forward is fine.
  4. In the top right corner, click New Project.
  5. On the right side, click the machine button (it may say Device Library) and pick MetalFab CNC Cutter.
  6. On the left toolbar, click the + button (it says Import when you hover). Click Desktop, pick your DXF, click Open.
  7. Click your tag. Check W and H in the top bar: 2 in (50.8 mm) or less.

You got it if the right side says MetalFab CNC Cutter and your tag is on the canvas at 2 in or less.

The right side shows a different machine
Click it again and pick MetalFab CNC Cutter. Not the apparel printer, not the P3.
It asks you to sign in
Look for Skip or the X. You do not need an account. If it will not let you past, raise your hand.
The tag is tiny or huge
A units mix-up. Click it, make sure the lock between W and H is on, and type the width you drew in SolidWorks.
The DXF is not on the Desktop
Go back to SolidWorks and do the Save As in step 3 again, clicking Desktop.
The numbers are in mm, you want inches
Settings, General Settings, Unit, pick inches. Or just use 50.8 mm.
5Add your name and one more thingxTool Studio
  • T is text. Click the tag, then double-click the text to type.
  • Images: black and white only. The laser has one shade.
Every click
  1. On the left toolbar, click T (Text). Then click on your tag where you want the words.
  2. Double-click the text and type. Click anywhere off the text when you are done.
  3. To change the font, click the text once, open the font list in the top bar, and pick one.
  4. To resize it, drag a corner handle, or type W and H in the top bar. Keep it inside the tag.
  5. For an image, find one online. Right-click it, choose Save image as, and save it to Downloads.
  6. Back in xTool Studio, click the + (Import) button again. Open Downloads, pick the image, click Open.
  7. It often comes in huge. Drag a corner handle inward until it fits on the tag.

You got it if your name and one more thing sit inside the tag, clear of the edge and the holes.

The image comes out as a gray blob
It is a photo, and the laser cannot do shading. Pick a black and white silhouette or a logo with thick lines.
The text went somewhere else
Click it once and drag it onto the tag.
You typed and nothing showed up
It is off screen. Hold Ctrl and scroll out to find it.
6Cut the shape, engrave the rest, center outwardxTool Studio
Cut
Your DXF: the outline and every hole.
Engrave
Fills it in. Your name and image. Images can only engrave.
Score
Traces a line. An option for text.

Must do: turn on cut from center outward, so every hole cuts before the outline. If the outline cuts first, the tag drops loose and the holes cut in the wrong place.

Every click
  1. Click your DXF shape. On the right side, choose Cut. Check that the outline and every hole turned to Cut.
  2. Click your name. Choose Engrave to fill the letters, or Score to trace their outline.
  3. Click your image, if you have one. It can only be Engrave.
  4. Set the cut order to cut from center outward, the way Mr. Pina shows it in the demo. The holes cut first and the outline cuts last.
  5. In the bottom right, click Process. If a message pops up, click Continue.
  6. Click Preview processing path, then press play and watch the order.

You got it if in the preview the marking happens, then every hole cuts, and the outline cuts last.

Only part of the shape turned to Cut
Drag a box around the whole tag shape to select all of it, then choose Cut again.
Your name is set to Cut
The letters fall out and the middles of O, A and B drop out too. Fine if you mean it. If not, change it to Engrave.
The outline cuts before a hole
Cut from center outward is not on. Close the preview, turn it on, and preview again. Do not upload until the outline cuts last.
A hole does not cut in the preview
It is not set to Cut. Click it and choose Cut.
You want to fix something
Close the preview to get back to your design, change it, and preview again.
7Save, then upload both filesxTool Studio
  • In xTool Studio: Ctrl + S, Save locally, Desktop, dogtag-yourname.
  • Upload the SolidWorks part and the xTool file in the boxes on this page. The DXF goes nowhere.
Every click
  1. In xTool Studio, press Ctrl + S. Click Save locally, not the cloud.
  2. Click Desktop on the left. Name it dogtag- and your name. Click Save.
  3. On this page, in the SolidWorks part box, click Choose file, click Desktop, pick the file with the red SolidWorks icon, and click Open.
  4. Wait for that box to turn green.
  5. In the xTool file box, click Choose file, click Desktop, pick the file with the xTool icon, and click Open. Or drag each file from your desktop onto its box.
  6. Wait for both boxes to turn green and say Handed in.
  7. Changed something afterward? Save it again, then Replace file in that box.

You got it if both boxes are green and show your file names.

It says that is the wrong file
Your Desktop has three dogtag files. The SolidWorks part ends in .SLDPRT and has the red icon. The xTool file ends in .xs and has the xTool icon. The DXF is not uploaded.
Your file is not on the Desktop
It saved somewhere else. Save it again and click Desktop this time.
It stays on Uploading
Wait 10 seconds, then try again. Still stuck? Raise your hand.
