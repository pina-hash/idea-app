  1. Calipers: close the jaws, press zero, check the screen reads in, not mm.
  2. Across: open the jaws over the two blade tips farthest apart.
  3. Height: stand it on its tip and measure up to the top of the collar.
  4. Weight: put the whole fight-ready Blade on the scale, set to grams.

    3. On your copy, click the TEMPLATE tag and press Delete.
