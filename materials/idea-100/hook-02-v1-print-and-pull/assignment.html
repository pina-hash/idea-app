A Step 1 of 17

Save your hook as a STEP file.

  1. In SolidWorks: File, Save As.
  2. Save as type: STEP AP214 (*.step;*.stp). Never STL.
  3. Save it in your own folder. Same name is fine.
More about this step

A multi-part hook: one STEP file per piece. A STEP file keeps your exact shape and your units; an STL is a mesh of triangles that loses both.

A Step 2 of 17

Drag it into Bambu Studio.

  1. Open Bambu Studio and drag the STEP file onto the plate.
  2. Multi-part: every piece onto the same plate.
  3. Check the size: a full-size hook is about 80 to 150 mm long.
More about this step

Much smaller or larger means the wrong file or the wrong units. Undo, export the STEP from your V1 part again (step 1), and drag that one in.

A Step 3 of 17

Set the printer, nozzle, plate and filament.

  1. Printer and nozzle: Bambu Lab X1 Carbon 0.6 nozzle. Every X1 Carbon here has a 0.6.from the table below.X1 Carbon: Bambu Lab X1 Carbon 0.6 nozzle. H2D: the table below.
  2. Plate type: the plate in your printer. Its name is printed on it.
  3. Filament: Generic ABS, always. Every spool here is HIPS.
  4. Change nothing else.
More about this step

The nozzle in the file must match the nozzle in the printer, or the printer refuses the file. The wrong plate type means the wrong bed temperature: the part will not stick, and the printer can stop.

A Step 4 of 17

Lay it flat.

  1. Select the hook. Press Lay on face on the top toolbar.
  2. Click its big flat side. Every piece, if you have more than one.
  3. Drag it fully onto the plate.

Flat, the pull runs along the layers: the strong way. Your preview screenshot in step 7 shows it.

B Step 5 of 17

Slice it and read the weight.

  1. Press Slice plate (top right).
  2. Read the filament weight in grams. That is your hook at full size.
  3. Multi-part: every piece on the plate. A piece printed twice goes on twice (Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V).

B Step 6 of 17

Scale it to 50 percent.

  1. Back to Prepare. Ctrl + A selects every piece.
  2. Scale tool on the top toolbar: uniform, 50 percent.
  3. Slice plate again. Every piece should read 50 percent.
  4. One plate, your hook only: nobody else’s part.
More about this step

Supports count. If the slicer added supports, their weight is in the estimate. On inspection day the scale weighs the hook with the supports removed.

  2. Look: solid walls there, or walls with infill between them?

D Step 10 of 17

Export the sliced file onto it.

  1. The small arrow beside Print plate (top right): Export plate sliced file.
  2. Click the carddrivecard or drive on the left. Stay at its top level, not in a folder.
  3. Name it with your last name, like SMITH-hook02-half. Save.
  4. Windows asks to replace a file? Press No: that is someone else’s. Add your first initial.

Only a sliced file prints. A STEP or project file does not show up on the printer.

D Step 11 of 17

Eject it, then pull it out.

  1. File Explorer: right-click the carddrivecard or drive, Eject.
  2. Wait until it leaves the list. Then take it out.
  3. X1 Carbon Card out of the adapter. Adapter back on the table.
E Step 12 of 17

Check the printer and seat the plate.

  1. The printer is not busy and the plate is empty and clean. A print still running is not yours: ask your Print Tech for another printer.
  2. Plate flat on the magnets, back edge against the stops, printed side up.
  3. A thin, even layer of glue stick where the hook will sit.
More about this step

If the printer later says Build plate marker not detected, the plate is crooked or pulled toward the door: seat it again.

E Step 13 of 17

Check the filament.

  1. On the screen: the Filament page. Find the HIPS.
  2. Its slot must say Generic ABS. Anything else: tap the slot, pick Generic ABS, confirm.
  3. X1 Carbon In the AMS on top, or on the spool holder at the back.
  4. H2D On the side you used in step 8.
  5. Not loaded? Load or change the filament is under these steps. Or ask your Print Tech.

Our spools have no Bambu tag, so the printer cannot tell what they are.

E Step 14 of 17

Put the carddrivecard or drive back and start the print.

  1. Card back in the slot beside the screen.Drive back in the USB port, top left.Card or drive back in the printer. It fits one way. Never force it.
  2. X1 Carbon Print Files, the card, your file. Leave the calibration boxes on.
  3. Enable AMS: on for the AMS, off for the back spool holder. The slot points at the HIPS. Print.
  4. H2D Print Files, the USB drive, your file. Calibration on auto, Next.
  5. Pick the HIPS slot on your side and start. Cannot pick it? Your file is for the other side: step 8.
E Step 15 of 17

Watch the first layer, then close the door.

  1. Stay a few minutes for the first layer.
  2. Good: flat, even lines stuck to the plate.
  3. Curling, dragging or blobs: press Stop and tell your Print Tech.
  4. Door and top closed while it prints.

HIPS gives off fumes when hot, and the closed printer holds the heat it needs.

E Step 16 of 17

Mark it on the print board.

  1. Tell your Print Tech it is running.
  2. Write it on the print board: printer, your name, start time.
  3. Half size takes about 20 to 30 minutes.
F Step 17 of 17

When it finishes: cool, flex, clean up.

  1. Wait until the plate is cool to the touch. The bed runs near 90°C.
  2. Lift the plate off, bend it gently: the part pops off. A scraper only if you must, never a knife.
  3. Peel off the purge line. Plate back against the stops.
  4. Your pieces in your bin, with your name. The carddrivecard or drive stays in the printer.
Load or change the filament Only if the HIPS is not already loaded. Ask your Print Tech the first time.

X1 Carbon In the AMS on top

  1. Open the AMS lid and put the spool in a slot.
  2. Push the grey tab in front of the feeder and feed about 2 cm of filament in. The AMS takes it from there.
  3. On the screen, tap the slot and set it to Generic ABS. It will not do this by itself: our spools have no Bambu tag.
  4. To take it out: tap Unload, then push the feeder tab forward and pull the filament out by hand.

X1 Carbon On the spool holder at the back

  1. If filament from the AMS is in the toolhead, unload it to the AMS first.
  2. Put the spool on the back holder. Cut the filament end at a 45 degree angle.
  3. Push it into the back tube until it reaches the toolhead.
  4. On the screen: Filament page, tap the spool holder, set it to Generic ABS, tap Load, and push the filament by hand when it asks.
  5. Let it push plastic out until the new color is clean.
  6. When you start the print, turn Enable AMS off.

H2D In the AMS 2 Pro

  1. Put the spool in a slot and feed the filament in; the AMS pre-loads it.
  2. The AMS feeds one side of the printer: the upper tube at the back is the right nozzle, the lower tube is the left.
  3. On the screen, tap the slot and set it to Generic ABS. It will not do this by itself: our spools have no Bambu tag.
  4. Pick the slot and tap Load, or just start a print that uses it.
  5. If you stop a print yourself, tap Unload: it does not unload on its own then.

H2D On the external spool holder

  1. On the screen, edit the filament: set it to Generic ABS and the color, then Confirm.
  2. Put the spool on its holder the way the filament winds.
  3. Push the filament into the tube until it stops and the green light comes on.
  4. Tap Load. While it heats, push the filament again.
  5. When plastic comes out smoothly, tap Filament Extruded; Continue. Nothing came out? Not Extruded Yet; Retry and push again.
  6. To take it out: tap Unload, turn the spool back as it comes out, and tuck the end into the spool's hole.
Something went wrong Find your problem. Each one says what to do.
My hook is the wrong size in Bambu Studio.

You probably dragged in the wrong file. Undo, export a STEP file from your V1 part again (step 1), and drag that one in. Still wrong? Check the part’s units in SolidWorks.

My hook floats, hangs off the plate, or has a red outline.

Select it, use Lay on face, then drag it fully onto the plate. The Arrange button on the top toolbar can place it for you.

The slice shows a warning about floating parts or supports.

Part of the hook is printed over air. Turn it flat on its biggest face first. If the warning stays, the slicer needs supports: check Enable support in the Support tab on the left.

My card or drive does not show up on the computer.

Push it all the way in, or try another port. A microSD card needs its adapter. Still nothing? Tell your Print Tech; do not borrow someone else’s card.

There is no card or drive in the printer.

Someone else is using it. Wait for it to come back, or ask your Print Tech for another printer. Never take one out of another printer.

My file is not on the printer’s list.

You saved the STEP file or the project, not the sliced file: go back to step 10 and use Export plate sliced file. Or it is inside a folder: save it at the top level of the card or drive. Or the card is not pushed all the way in.

H2D: I cannot pick my filament when I start.

Your file is sliced for the other nozzle. Go back to step 8: Regroup filament, Custom, move it to the loaded side, slice, export again.

The printer says the nozzle is not matched with the slicing file.

Your file was sliced for a different nozzle than the one in the printer. Every X1 Carbon here is 0.6: pick Bambu Lab X1 Carbon 0.6 nozzle.Ask your Print Tech which nozzle is on your side and set it from the table in step 3.Set the nozzle again from step 3. Slice, export again, and restart.

The printer says the filament type does not match.

The spool’s slot on the printer is not set to Generic ABS. Our spools have no Bambu tag, so the printer never sets it by itself. On the screen’s Filament page, tap the HIPS slot, pick Generic ABS, confirm, and start again. Your file stays Generic ABS too.

It says Build plate marker not detected, or the plate type does not match.

Take the plate out and seat it again, flat and pushed to the back stops. If it is the plate type, set the right one in Bambu Studio, slice, export again.

The first layer will not stick, or the corners lift.

Press Stop. Let it cool, wash the plate with dish soap and water, dry it, glue it, and start again. Fingerprints are the usual cause.

It turned into a mess of loose strands, or it paused itself.

The printer watches for this and may pause on its own. Press Stop, clear the plate once it is cool, and tell your Print Tech before you start again.

It ran out of filament, or the AMS shows an error.

Leave it and tell your Print Tech. Never pull filament out of a printer that is running.

The part will not come off the plate.

It is still warm, so wait longer. Then flex the plate from different sides. A scraper, carefully, only if it is still stuck.

I lost the card or drive.

Tell Mr. Pina now. The sooner, the more likely it turns up.

Third class, then any time 15 pts

Stage 3 done. Test 1 is in. Keep pulling and logging until the tournament.
