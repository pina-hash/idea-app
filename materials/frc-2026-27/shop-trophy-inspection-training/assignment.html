Self-guided. No machines needed: just your five parts and a caliper. Do one part at a time, as many sessions as you like. Everything saves as you type. You are graded on the inspection, not on whether your parts pass.

How to measure not graded, read this once
  • Watch Measurement and Inspection above first. It shows all of this on the real tools.
  • Zero first. Wipe the jaws, close them, press zero. Every session.
  • Outside sizes (lengths, widths, the shaft): outside jaws, square to the part, snug but not squeezed.
  • Holes: the small inside jaws. Rock gently and take the largest reading.
  • Depths: the thin rod that slides out of the end of the caliper.
  • Micrometer: turn the ratchet, not the barrel, until it clicks.
  • Thread checks: a 1/4-20 screw, by hand. Never force it.
  • Measure twice. If the two readings disagree, take a third and say so in Remarks.
  • Fresh edges can be sharp. Hold parts by their faces.
