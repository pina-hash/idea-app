Shop Trophy: Machine Training

Five machines. Five parts. They bolt together into a trophy you keep. You are trained on a machine when you have made a part on it and can say what it did.

Work one station, come back here, and fill in that station. Nothing here has to be done in one sitting and nothing has to be done in order. Fill in what you have done.

The drawing standardnot graded

You get the model file for each part. Before you make that part, you draw it on paper.

The standard: someone who has never seen the model could make the part from your drawing. Every dimension needed to manufacture it. Every feature. Anything missing is something they would have to guess, and a guess is a wrong part.

The topper is different. There is no model. You design it yourself in the xTool software. Two rules: 2.5 inches maximum across, and a 1/4 inch hole dead center. Everything else is yours. Sketch it on paper first, same standard.

Before you touch a machinenot graded

Same five questions at every station. In your notebook, before you start.

1. What am I making? Sketch it with the dimensions.
2. What are the steps, in order?
3. What tool and what settings for each step?
4. Where do I stop and measure?
5. What is most likely to go wrong here?

Phones at the machinesnot graded

Never the same person. Whoever is running the machine does not touch the phone at any point.

The phone stays off the machine and out of the hazard area. Never on the table, the ways, or inside the enclosure.

You should be able to turn your head and see it. If you have to lean, reach, or step to see it, move it.

Phones come out for the station video and nothing else.
