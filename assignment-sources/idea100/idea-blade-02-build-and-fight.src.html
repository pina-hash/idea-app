Build

Build your Blade

  1. Body printed? Oven mitt on: plate onto the glass table till the print crackles free.
  2. Not printed yet? Start it first, it takes time: open the printing steps below.
  3. Blade: your team's new blade, plus any leftover blades you want.
  4. Stack it in your assembly's order: tip bolt, body, blades, hex core, collar.
  5. Tighten the collar so nothing slides.
  6. Hitting edges lead the spin (counterclockwise from above). Flip any blade whose edge trails.
  7. Spin it by hand. Rubbing or loose? Fix it now.

Every part on the hex, spinning clean in your fingers.

Build

Measure it

  1. Calipers: close the jaws, press ZERO, check the screen says in.
  2. A · Across: the two blade tips farthest apart.
  3. B · Height: hold it upright, one jaw on the tip, the other on top of the collar.
  4. C · Collar height, then D, the bare hex above it.
  5. Weight: the whole fight-ready Blade on the scale, in grams. 680 g is 1.5 lb.

Every row under the boxes says Inside.

  • Your numbers are checked here against the rules.

Build

Get it inspected

  1. Bring the Blade and this page to Mr. Pina. He checks it.
  2. Over a rule? Fix it, then come back.
  3. Pick your result only after he has checked it.

A number over a rule is a fix, not a failed grade.

Over 1.5 lb (680 g)Take off a leftover blade, or reprint the body with less infill.
Over 3.00 in tallLower the collar, or run a thinner body.
Under 0.50 in of bare hexMove the collar down the hex.
Collar over 0.50 inSwap it for a shorter collar.
Over 5.00 in acrossSwap the longest blade for a smaller leftover.

Mr. Pina has checked it and your pick is saved.

Slide

Make your team's slide

  1. One slide per team: one person types, everyone checks it.
  2. Open the deck. Pick a TEMPLATE slide, dark or light. Right-click it in the slide list, press Duplicate slide.
  3. Drag your copy to the end of the list.
  4. On your copy, delete the TEMPLATE tag and type over every box. Your numbers are below.
  5. Insert, Image, Upload from computer: your Blade photo, filling the frame.
  6. Type your slide's number below. Next step: the screenshot.
