V3 · 35 min

Build V3 at the red

V3 is the hook you take to the final. Build it from what the plot showed: material where V2 went red, grams from where it was blue.

  1. Open V2. File, Save As: V3_ and your last name, in your Armory folder. V2 stays as it is.
  2. Make the change from your V3 plan in Hook 03. Rebuild: no red or yellow in the tree.
  3. Click the face or feature you changed so it highlights. Screenshot the whole window: V3_ at the top.

V3 · 10 min

Measure V3

Material at the red can close the opening or shrink the top hole. Evaluate tab, Measure. Inches, three decimals. Anything outside a rule gets fixed before it prints.

V3 · 15 min

Weigh V3 on your settings

  1. File, Save As, STEP AP214.
  2. Bambu Studio: your printer and nozzle, Generic ABS, Textured PEI, Enable support, Auto orient.
  3. Choose your wall loops and infill: more is stronger and heavier.
  4. Slice plate at full size. The slicer's weight, supports included, is the rule: at or under 0.110 lb (50.0 g).

V3 · 20 min

Make your call

Before the final, say what V3 will do. You are graded on the reason, not on being right. Your call locks when this post closes, the night before the final.

  • A rough guide: your half-size pulls are in Hook 02. Full size has four times the cross section, so about four times the load, if it breaks the same way.
  • Rough because walls do not scale: the nozzle sets their width. Different walls or infill from your test copy change it too.
  • Your plot says where the stress piles up.

Final print · 25 min

Print V3

  1. Slice V3 at full size on your settings. Screenshot the plate with the weight.
  2. Export plate sliced file onto the printer's card or drive, named LASTNAME-V3. Never press Print plate.
  3. On the printer: HIPS slot set to Generic ABS, Print Files, your file, Print.
  4. Watch the first layer, close the door. A full-size hook takes a while.
  5. Done before the bell: oven mitt, plate onto the glass table until it crackles free. Remove every support.

Still printing at the bell? Leave it on the printer, door closed. Take it off at the start of the final.
