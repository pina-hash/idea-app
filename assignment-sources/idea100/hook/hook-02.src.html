V1 · 50 min

Finish V1

Today it becomes a whole hook: finished enough to print. It does not have to be legal yet.

  1. Build it from your own sketch, with your 3D feature from Hook 01.
  2. Rebuild (Ctrl+B). The tree should have no red or yellow.
  3. Ctrl+S often.
  4. Screenshot the whole window: your part, the file name at the top, the tree on the left.

A whole hook, and a tree with no red or yellow marks.

Tools you may want
  • Measure (Evaluate tab): Click two circular edges for center to center, or one edge for its diameter. On a hook: Every number in the table.
  • Offset Entities (Sketch tab): Copies an outline a set distance inside or outside itself. On a hook: A raised rim: offset inward, then cut a pocket inside it.
  • Boss from a face (Features tab): Click a face, sketch on it, extrude new material up from it. On a hook: Thicker bosses around the holes, or a thicker bowl.
  • Extruded Cut (Features tab): Removes material through a sketch. On a hook: Pockets where the load does not go.
  • Fillet (Features tab): Rounds an edge to a radius. On a hook: Every sharp inside corner, where cracks start.
  • Chamfer (Features tab): Cuts an edge to a flat bevel. On a hook: A down-facing edge: 45 degrees prints without support.

Print · 15 min

Set it up in Bambu Studio

  1. Pick a free printer now and stay with it: you slice for that printer.
  2. SolidWorks: File, Save As, type STEP AP214, your folder. Never STL. Multi-part: one STEP per piece, all onto one plate.
  3. Bambu Studio: Create new project (the cube with a plus), your STEP file.
  4. Printer: X1 Carbon, 0.6 mm, flow Standard. H2D: on its screen, Settings, Nozzle and extruder says which nozzle is on the HIPS side. Set exactly that.
  5. Filament: Generic ABS (every spool here is HIPS). Plate: Textured PEI.
  6. Enable support, then Auto orient. The hook lies flat, so the pull runs along the layers.

Print · 10 min

Weigh it, then halve it

  1. Slice plate. Read the filament weight in grams: your hook at full size.
  2. Back to Prepare. Ctrl+A, then Scale: uniform, 50 percent.
  3. Slice again and read it. One plate, your hook only.

Two weights: full size, then about one sixth to one eighth of it at half size.

Print · 25 min

Print it

  1. Card or drive out of your printer: X1 Carbon microSD beside the screen, into an adapter from the tables between the printers. H2D: USB, top left.
  2. The arrow beside Print plate: Export plate sliced file, onto the card or drive, top level, named LASTNAME-hook02-half. Never press Print plate.
  3. Eject it, back into its printer.
  4. On the printer: Filament page, tap the HIPS slot, it must say Generic ABS. Pick it if not.
  5. Print Files, your file, Print. Watch the first layer, then close the door.
  6. Done: oven mitt, plate onto the glass table until the print crackles free. Scrap in a green bin, plate back against the stops.

The pull · 15 min

Pull it

  1. Safety glasses on. Stand where the rig operator (Mr. Pina, or the student he names) tells you.
  2. Watch where it breaks. That is the real result.
  3. Copy your load from the score sheet at the rig.
  4. Keep every piece in your bin. You need them for V2.
  5. Photograph the break: close, the broken face in focus. On your phone: open this post, Take photo.
