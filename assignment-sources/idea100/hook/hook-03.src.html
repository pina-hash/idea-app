V2 · 15 min

Pick your fix

Your half-size copy from Hook 02 broke somewhere. That spot is the whole brief: add material there, and take grams from places that showed no strain.

Test 1
Where did Test 1 break? Never broke: where it stretched most (white marks).

Pick where it broke, and the usual fix for that spot shows here.

Thicken the ring

  1. Edit the sketch and make the outer circle of the top ring bigger.
  2. The hole stays 1.000 in or more. Only the ring around it grows.

The pull runs through the ring on both sides of the hole. More ring, less stress in it.

Round the inside corner

  1. Find the sharp inside corner where it broke: under the ring, or where the shank turns into the bowl.
  2. Make that Fillet bigger, or add one.

A sharp inside corner piles the stress up in one spot, and cracks start there. A bigger round spreads it out.

Widen the shank

  1. Edit the sketch and move the shank's outside line out.
  2. Keep the opening 0.500 in or more.

The shank is pulled straight. Its stress is the load divided by its area: more width, less stress.

Make the bowl deeper

  1. Edit the sketch and make the bowl's outer circle bigger, moving it down.
  2. The inside stays 1.000 in or more; the opening stays 0.500 in or more.

The bowl bends, and in bending the depth counts twice. A tip that pulled open is the bowl bending too far.

Give the joint more grip

  1. Make the keys bigger, or add another one.
  2. Round the inside corners of each key. It still opens and closes by hand in 10 s.

A joint is where the pull passes through less material than anywhere else. Give it more.

The fix is a starting point. How much to change, and which number, is your call: you will write it in step 4.

V2 · 20 min

Weigh it and say what it cost

  1. Same as Hook 02: Save As STEP, into Bambu Studio, X1 Carbon 0.6 mm or your H2D side, Generic ABS, Slice plate.
  2. Read the weight at full size, not 50 percent.
  3. Over 0.110 lb (50.0 g)? It cannot print for the final. Take grams from a place your test showed was fine: no crack, no white marks.

Material is never free. A gram added at the break is worth more than a gram anywhere else.

Done early? Print V2 at half size like Hook 02 and pull it. Log it under More pulls in Hook 02. Every pull before the final is free information.

Simulate · 20 min

Turn on Simulation, split two faces

  1. Open V2 (press R for recent files). No Simulation tab? SOLIDWORKS Add-Ins tab, click SOLIDWORKS Simulation.
  2. Front Plane, Sketch. Draw a circle about the size of the rig's dowel (the round bar your hook hangs from), about halfway across the inside of the top loop. Eyeball it: no dimensions.
  3. Features tab, the arrow under Curves, Split Line. Top box: your circle. Projection. Second box: the inside face of the top loop. Green check.
  4. Again in the bowl: a smaller circle where the rope rests, Split Line on the inside face of the bowl.

A small face of its own in the top loop and another in the bowl, each with a line around it.

Simulate · 10 min

Run it

  1. Run This Study. It meshes the hook, then solves. Busy for a minute or two is normal: never close SolidWorks.
  2. A notification pops up when it finishes: click No. It just finishes this study.
  3. Under Results, double-click Stress.
  4. Screenshot the hook and the color scale.
