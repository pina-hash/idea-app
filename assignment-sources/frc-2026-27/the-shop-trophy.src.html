The Shop Trophy

Done when: you know how a station runs, which video and model go with each part, and you have your five plan questions ready.

5 stations · 5 partsNot graded

Read this once: the same at every station

How it works

Watch. Plan. Cut.
  1. Watch your station's video before your rotation opens. Not at the machine.
  2. Plan in your notebook before you touch anything. Five questions, in this post.
  3. Cut with the video playing. Your partner holds the phone. You run the machine.
  4. No plan, no machine. Your notebook gets checked before you start.
Two sessions per station

Your group gets two sessions at each station. In those two sessions, every person in the group leaves with their own part. How you get there is up to the group.

Five rotations. Five stations. Five parts. They assemble into your trophy, and you take it home.

Bottom to top

What you are making

BaseCut and engraved. Your name goes on it. Add a logo or an image if you have time. On the H2D laser.
ColumnAluminum box tube, squared up, then two holes: one tapped 1/4-20, one 1/4 inch through. On the manual mill.
PlatformPolycarbonate, cut on the router. On the Axiom CNC router.
ShaftHex stock, turned, tapped 1/4-20 at both ends, rounded on one end. On the manual lathe.
TopperSteel, your design. On the xTool MetalFab.

All the threads are 1/4-20. The five part models (p1 to p5, SolidWorks) are attached to this post, below it.

The topper is different

There is no model. You design it yourself in the xTool software. Two rules: 2.5 inches maximum width or diameter, and a 1/4 inch hole dead center. Everything else is yours. Sketch it on paper first, same standard.

One per part, on paper, before you make it

The drawing

You get the model file for each part. Before you make that part, you draw it on paper.

The standard: someone who has never seen the model could make the part from your drawing. Every dimension needed to manufacture it. Every feature. If something is missing, they would have to guess, and a guess is a wrong part.

You end with five drawings, one per part.

In your notebook, before every station

The five plan questions, and phones

  1. What am I making? Sketch it with the dimensions.
  2. What are the steps, in order?
  3. What tool and what settings for each step?
  4. Where do I stop and measure?
  5. What is most likely to go wrong here?
One holds the phone, one runs the machine
  • Never the same person. The person running the machine does not touch the phone at any point.
  • The phone stays off the machine and out of the hazard area. Never on the table, the ways, or inside the enclosure.
  • You should be able to turn your head and see it. If you have to lean, reach, or step to see it, move it.
  • Phones come out for the station video and nothing else.
