Module 1.1

Learn how an FRC match and event work

30 min

You will: Explain alliances, the match clock, ranking and playoffs; find the 2027 event.

  1. Learn the match: 2 alliances of 3 robots, red and blue; the schedule sets partners.
  2. Learn the 2026 REBUILT clock (until Jan 9, 2027): AUTO 20 s, a pause, TELEOP 2:20.
  3. Split TELEOP: TRANSITION SHIFT 10 s, SHIFTS 1 to 4 at 25 s each, END GAME 30 s.
  4. Follow the event: practice, qualification matches for ranking points, then playoffs.
  5. Look up Team 5669 on The Blue Alliance: CALAS, Mar 19 to 21, 2027, Week 3.

You can name both alliances, the 2026 match phases with their times, and the team's 2027 event and dates.

More detail
  • A match has 6 robots. In qualification matches the schedule assigns your partners.
  • In AUTO the robot runs its own code. The short pause after it is while AUTO is scored. A full match is 2:40.
  • Alliance selection forms 8 alliances of 3 teams for the playoffs.
  • CALAS is CA District Los Angeles, at DaVinci HS, El Segundo.
  • 2027 dates: kickoff Jan 9 (game: BIOCORE), Week 0 Feb 20, CALAS Mar 19 to 21, FIRST Championship Apr 28 to May 1 in Houston.
  • What FIRST has said about BIOCORE: one type of scoring element, and robots have a holding limit above one. Everything else waits for Jan 9.

Module 1.2

Scout one robot in the Techmen app

45 min

You will: Record one robot's match in the scouting portal with honest counts and a useful note.

  1. Open the scouting portal in the Techmen app and open the event while you have signal, so it works offline.
  2. Tap Practice and finish See it. Record it. and Count it, or leave it blank before your first match.
  3. On Scout this, check the event, match and team number before the match. Sit down 2 matches early.
  4. Watch only your robot: tap +1 or +5 as it scores, -1 to fix a miscount, Saw zero if it did nothing.
  5. Add one note with a time and an action, tap Save observation, then confirm it synced.

Your observation shows as synced and appears under that team in Teams, with the match and your note.

More detail
  • Every member scouts. One scout per robot, every match, practice matches included.
  • Open the event before you walk into the venue.
  • In 2026 the count fields are FUEL.
  • View blocked: leave it Not recorded or pick Did not see.
  • Keep the note about the robot only: "At 1:14 stopped near the loading area; cause not visible."
  • Saved on this device is not the same as synced to the team.

Module 2.1

Pre-scout an event with Statbotics, TBA and the app

45 min

You will: Size up every team before an event with Statbotics EPA, TBA and the app's data.

  1. Open the team list on FRC Events or The Blue Alliance. CALAS holds up to 40 teams.
  2. Look up each team's EPA on Statbotics, split into auto, teleop and endgame.
  3. In the Techmen app scouting portal, open the event, then Teams to see each EPA.
  4. Tap Download spreadsheet for CSV files of every observation and every team.
  5. Write 3 lines per opponent: strongest EPA part, what scouts watch, what you don't know.

One sheet with every team at the event, its EPA and parts, and one question for the scouts.

More detail
  • EPA (Expected Points Added) estimates the points a team adds to its alliance.
  • Read EPA as an estimate. Early in a season it leans on last season's data; by a team's second event it is mostly current-season.
  • Each team card shows the EPA with the date it was fetched, marked "Not an official robot score."
  • Sort the CSV files in Google Sheets.
  • Scouting leads: in Manage event, enter the TBA event key, tap Preview event import, check the teams and schedule, then save the import.

Module 2.2

Build a pick list from evidence

50 min

You will: Build a ranked, defensible pick list in the app from scouting data and EPA.

  1. List at least 24 teams (8 alliances x 3). Scout your own team too; teams overrate themselves.
  2. Sort by your single most important stat, then compare teams 2 at a time to fix the order.
  3. In the Techmen app scouting portal, open Pick lists and narrow the field with Find teams.
  4. Place each team in Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3 or Do not pick, with a robot reason for every Do not pick.
  5. Bring your list to the pick list meeting, held the night before alliance selection.

Your saved list has 24 or more teams in tiers, each placement backed by at least one observation.

More detail
  • Find teams filters: Minimum EPA, pit capability, match stats.
  • A Do not pick reason names the robot, such as "tipped in 3 of 8 matches."
  • Use Compare three teams for close calls. Read the preview; nothing changes until you apply it.
  • Count rates honestly: only matches someone actually saw go in the math. Did not see stays visible, but out of the rate.
  • The team master list uses each scout's median tier and rank.
  • Karthik's Effective FIRST Strategies is 60 slides.

Module 2.3

Run kickoff strategic design with the worksheets

90 min

You will: Turn a new manual into cited facts, a strategy and a locked MoSCoW priority list.

  1. Make your own copy of the Kickoff Worksheet and join a strike team of 3 to 4.
  2. Fill Parts 1 and 2 first, citing the manual section for every fact.
  3. List every scoring method and its points in auto, teleop and endgame; dismiss none.
  4. Fill Part 3 (strategy), then Part 4 (MoSCoW), then present your Must have list.

Every worksheet fact has a manual section, and the Must have list is the simplest robot that scores real value.

More detail
  • Copy the worksheet with File, Make a copy. It is the Techmen template.
  • Role focus: build leads on field zones, robot size and scoring; programming leads on vision targets, AprilTag IDs and auto; strategy leads on ranking points, cycle times and traffic.
  • Parts 1 and 2 cover the field, game pieces, scoring and penalties. Do them before any strategy.
  • Do not dismiss any scoring method as worth too little.
  • Part 4 sorts tasks into Must, Should, Could, Won't. Weigh each task's value against its cost to build and do it.
  • Present the Must have list at the team sync. After the MoSCoW lock, no new features.
  • Programmers also fill the Software and Controls Kickoff Worksheet.
  • After the lock, every prototype gets a Prototype Test Run Sheet (3 variables max) and an entry in the Prototype Validation Log.

Module 3.1

Plan alliance selection and match strategy at an event

60 min

You will: Turn scouting into match plans and lead the pick list meeting.

  1. Label each qualification match favored, toss-up or likely to lose.
  2. Predict with Statbotics: add each alliance's 3 EPAs, then check against scouting.
  3. In the scouting portal, tap Brief match, review, then Export printable brief.
  4. Send stands-to-pits notes: the team, the match, what you saw and one question.
  5. Run the pick list meeting the night before alliance selection, from the master list.

A printed brief for each match and a pick list every scout can defend with evidence.

More detail
  • Set a ranking point milestone for the event.
  • An alliance's predicted score is the sum of its 3 robots' EPAs.
  • The brief is the Drive-team match brief in the Techmen app: Brief match, then Confirm lineup and review brief, write the coach plan, then Export printable brief.
  • Example note: "Team 9001 stopped in Q12 at 0:40; did repairs change their climb plan?"
  • In the meeting, debate close teams with evidence and keep Do not pick reasons about robots.
  • Plan playoff repairs: 2027 adds a 5-minute break between playoff rounds 2 and 3, and the break between rounds 3 and 4 grows to 10 minutes.
  • In Karthik's deck, also read Preparing for the Finals.

Module 3.2

Run scouting shifts at an event

60 min

You will: Plan, staff and adjust scouting so every robot is covered every match.

  1. Aim for one scout per robot, every match, practice matches included.
  2. Import the event in the Techmen app: Manage event, TBA event key, Preview event import, then save.
  3. Open Plan a scouting shift, fill it in, then tap Preview assignment plan.
  4. Check Uncovered robots is empty, save, and move Now on the field +1 each match.
  5. After each block, check every Check row in the pairs file from Download spreadsheet.

The plan preview shows zero uncovered robots and every scout can see their next match.

More detail
  • The scouting lead trains every member to scout.
  • Plan fields: Shift block (by match number) of 6 to 20 matches, Robot scouts, Scouts per robot (pairs need 12 or more scouts) and Super scouts per alliance (1 or 2).
  • Move Now on the field as each match starts. Shifts are match numbers, so a late field breaks nothing, and each scout sees when they are up.
  • A row is marked Check when counts are more than 10 FUEL apart, ratings more than 1 point apart, or a yes or no is split.
  • Teach new scouts with the Practice tab's 9 missions. Practice is preparation; official certification is awarded in IDEA Classroom.

The event is running 30 minutes late.Change nothing in the plan. Keep the Now panel on the real match number.
A scout left mid-block.Plan the rest of the block again with the scouts you have. Existing work is kept and uncovered robots show up.
I only have 9 scouts.Use one scout per robot (6 per match) and rotate the other 3 in. Pairs need 12.
