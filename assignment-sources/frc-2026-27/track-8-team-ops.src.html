Module 3.1

Draft the 2027 Impact Award essays

3 hr, over several sessions

You will: Plan and draft the three 2027 Impact essays in the new format.

  1. Open FRC Submitted Awards: submissions open Oct 29, 2026 at noon ET and close Feb 11, 2027 at 3 PM ET (noon Pacific).
  2. Write three equally weighted essays of up to 4,000 characters, no executive summary.
  3. Answer one prompt per essay: spread the FIRST Mission; impact on the FIRST community; inspire the STEM future.
  4. Build each essay as a 3-year overview, then depth on the most recent 12 to 18 months.
  5. Pull facts only from records: Techmen app hours, outreach logs, counts. No guesses.

Three essay drafts, each under 4,000 characters, each answering only its own prompt.

More detail
  • This is the 2027 format: three essays, no executive summary.
  • The third prompt, inspire the STEM future, focuses on the team's sustainability.
  • Read the updated Definitions on the FIA resources page, including the new term sustained.
  • Use 118's 2025 write-up as a model of data, not of structure. It uses the old executive summary format; move its kind of facts into the three prompts.
  • Check every essay: under 4,000 characters, no angle bracket characters (less-than or greater-than signs), and any AI use credited the way FIRST asks.
  • Ask Mr. Pina before using AI.

Module 3.3

Lead a judging interview

1.5 hr

You will: Prepare and run an Impact presentation and a pit interview that judges can score.

  1. Plan the 12-minute Impact interview: up to 7 minutes of presentation, then questions.
  2. Pick up to 3 student presenters; time a full run with setup and cut it to 7 minutes.
  3. Make one pit binder page per subsystem: a render, a one-sentence purpose, key specs.
  4. Log teams you meet in Meet a team in the Techmen app.
  5. Read the Technical Judges Tip Sheet, then run a mock interview with a teammate as judge.

A timed Impact run under 7 minutes and a binder page any team member can talk through.

More detail
  • Setup counts inside the 7 minutes; judge questions fill the rest of the 12.
  • One adult may watch the Impact interview silently. No recording.
  • In the pit, judges cannot see engineering by looking at the robot. The team must be able to describe its process.
  • Gracious Professionalism questions now ask about pre-season work with other FRC teams. Your Meet a team log helps you answer.
