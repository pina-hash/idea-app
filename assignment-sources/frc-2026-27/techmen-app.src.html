Module 1.3

Scout and study the teams

5 min

You will: Scout every match you are assigned and study the teams before alliance selection.

  1. Open Scouting for Beach Blitz and find your assigned matches.
  2. Charge your phone the night before. Bring a charger.
  3. Scout one robot per match: numbers plus a short note.
  4. Open Meet a team to read about the teams at the event.
  5. Alliance selection is about 11:25 AM on Nov 1. Have your notes in by then.

Module 2.1

Run your week

3 min

You will: Check the schedule, log your hours and see where you stand.

  1. Open Schedule: two team meetings a week are mandatory (80% attendance).
  2. Plan at least 2 hours a week of build or training.
  3. After every session, open Log hours the same day and write what you did.
  4. Open My hours to check your total.
  5. FLL volunteering is logged as volunteer hours (10 a year, FLL included).
