Three things first

Three things first
  1. You need all 15 right to pass. Miss one, you retake it.
  2. No machine until you pass this exam. Passing is what clears you for the shop.
  3. Bring your respirator to your first shop session. Without it you are not in the shop.

Follow along. Nothing on the exam is outside these 15 rules.

Rules 1 to 4

Gear

  • 1. Safety glasses on any time you are in the shop area.Not just when you are running something.
  • 2. Long hair tied back. Loose clothing tucked in. Jewelry off.Anything that hangs can get pulled in.
  • 3. Your respirator has to seal.If it feels loose, it is not protecting you. Fix it before you start.
  • 4. Every injury gets reported when it happens.Including the small ones.

Rules 5 to 7

Permission

  • 5. No machine until you pass the safety exam.Passing is what clears you for the shop. Watching a station video does not. Feeling ready is not the same as having passed.
  • 6. Never run a machine alone.No running machines in an empty shop, including open shop. Someone has to be there.
  • 7. Not sure if it is safe? Ask first.If you are not sure whether something is safe, stop and ask. Trying it slowly is still trying it.

Rules 8 to 10

At the machine

  • 8. Find the emergency stop before you turn it on.If you cannot stop it fast, you are not ready to start it.
  • 9. Never reach into a machine while it is running.Stop the machine first. Every time. That includes your hands, a tool, or a rag.
  • 10. New sound, new smell, new vibration? Stop.Tell your instructor. Do not finish the cut first.

Rules 11 to 13

Other people

  • 11. Do not interrupt someone who is running a machine.Wait until they are finished. One exception: their own partner reading them the station video. That partner is part of running the machine. Anyone else is an interruption.
  • 12. See something unsafe? Say something before they start.Any group, any machine. After they finish is too late to matter.
  • 13. Horseplay near a machine: tell them to stop.If they do not stop, tell your instructor.

Rules 14 and 15

The room

  • 14. Clean your station every day, even if it makes you late.The next person has to be able to work there.
  • 15. Finish early? Notebook, at your station.Stay at your station and start your notebook entry. Not another group's machine, and not a computer.

Before the shop

The exam, and phones at the machines

  • All 15 right to pass. Miss one and you retake it.
  • No devices, no notes, no talking.
  • Read each question all the way through. Some check whether you are paying attention.
  • Everything on the exam is on this page.
Phones at the machines
  • One person holds the phone. One person runs the machine. Never the same person.
  • The person running the machine does not touch the phone at any point.
  • The phone stays off the machine and out of the hazard area. Never on the table, the ways, or inside the enclosure.
  • You should be able to turn your head and see it. If you have to lean, reach, or step to see it, move it.
  • Phones come out for the station video and nothing else.
