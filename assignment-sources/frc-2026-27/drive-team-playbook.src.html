Your job on the drive team

The robot does the hard parts by itself. It knows where it is on the field, aims the turret, sets the hood and shooter speed, and only fires when the shot is lined up. Your job is to put it in the right place, at the right time, in the right mode.

Driver

Moves the robot, runs the intake and the climb. Listens to the coach for where to go.

Operator

Shoots and picks the modes. Watches the robot and tells the driver what it is doing ("Full", "Half", "Empty").

Coach

Watches the clock, the hubs, partners and refs. Makes the calls: "Come home", "Climb now", "Defend".

Technician

Runs the pre-match checks on the laptop so the driver and operator never have to.

Three rules for every match: hands off during auto, repeat the key word of every call (no repeat means it did not land), and feel the buzzes (see Buzzes) instead of looking down at the laptop.

Last updated Oct 28, 2026. When the robot code changes something you use, this page changes the same day and the "What changed" list at the end says what.

In the queue and on the field

Before every match

  1. Power on in the start pose (technician): turret on its HOME mark, intake pushed all the way UP, hood all the way DOWN, climber all the way DOWN. The robot counts from where it is at power on, so a wrong start pose throws off the whole match.
  2. Pick the auto on the dashboard ("Auto" chooser). Read it back to the coach. Any 3-piece auto: lower the intake to the floor before the match. Those autos start with the intake DOWN.
  3. Open Elastic, tab Drive Team. Pit Check must read ALL GO. Anything else is a list of what to fix.
  4. Check Code Version shows today's build, and the battery is fresh (Pit Check says if it is low).
  5. Both Xbox controllers plugged in: driver on port 0, operator on port 1 (Driver Station USB tab). Wrong order = the wrong person drives.
  6. On the field, place the robot where the auto starts, facing the way the auto expects.

Driver

How the robot drives

Field centric

"Away" on the stick is always away from our driver station. If the robot suddenly drives sideways or backwards from what you push, the gyro is off: point the robot straight away from our driver station and hold LB until the buzz.

Gentle at the start of the stick

The stick is curved: the first half of the push is slow and precise, the last part is fast. Small pushes make small moves.

Check yourself

It ramps, it does not jump

Speed changes are smoothed, so a full stop from top speed takes about a third of a second. Let off early before walls and robots.

Slow mode

Hold RB (or Start) for careful moves: lining up to climb, cleaning up next to a wall.

The robot helps while you shoot

  • While the operator holds RT to score, top speed drops to about 1.35 m/s (4.4 ft/s) so it keeps firing while you creep. Passing keeps full speed. Full speed anyway: hold the left stick click.
  • Let go of the sticks while scoring and the wheels lock in an X so a bump does not knock the shot off.
  • If the hub is behind the turret (it cannot turn all the way around), the robot turns itself just enough while a shot is wanted. Touch the turn stick and you take over.
The turret's blind spot

The turret turns about 300 degrees, not a full circle: the wires stop it. The blind spot is roughly front-left of the robot. When the hub sits there, the robot turns itself to fix it (only while shooting is wanted, never while climbing). If you are holding the turn stick, it is your job.

Operator and driver

How shooting works

  1. Teleop starts: auto-aim turns on by itself (operator feels one short buzz). The turret follows the hub, the hood stays down until a shot is wanted.
  2. Driver gets the robot inside our alliance zone. Operator holds RT.
  3. The robot spins up, raises the hood, and fires each ball the moment it is lined up. The operator controller buzzes steadily while it is ready.
  4. Keep holding RT through the whole volley. It pauses and resumes by itself.

Where we may score

Into OUR hub only while the robot's center is within 4.19 m (165 in) of our wall, which keeps the bumpers in our alliance zone. Outside it, a shot into our hub is a MAJOR foul (G407), so the robot will not do it.

Passing

Farther out, the robot passes: it lobs balls to a corner of our alliance zone. Deep in their zone it lobs to a spot in the neutral zone instead. If a hub is in the way, it waits: move sideways.

Closed hub

When the field says our hub is closed, it holds scoring shots and says "HUB CLOSED: opens in N s". It starts again a moment early so the first balls land as the hub opens. Passes are never held.

Vacuum and dump modes in detail
  • Vacuum (Operator Back): intake down, roller in, auto-aim on, fires by itself whenever ready. Scores inside our zone, passes home from the neutral zone. One long buzz on both controllers when it starts, two short when it ends.
  • Dump (Operator left stick click): for the trench with a full hopper: roller pushes balls OUT the front while the robot shoots. It lowers the intake itself. In the trench the hood stays down and firing pauses; in the neutral zone it passes home.
  • The drive team always wins: Driver RT/LT take the roller, Operator RT/B take the feeders, only while held. Vacuum picks back up after.
  • Turn them off: press the same button again, or Operator RB or Y, or Driver Y (defense).
  • While vacuum or dump is on, the drive is softer to save the battery. Driver holds the left stick click for full power.
The trench

The trench bar is low (22.25 in, 56.5 cm). Near a trench the robot keeps the hood all the way down by itself. Do not jog the hood up by hand near a trench: the hand jog is not limited. To shoot while parked beside a trench, the operator presses the right stick click (trench shot).

Driver

Defense mode

Driver Y turns defense on (one long buzz). The robot turns auto-aim and vacuum off, raises the intake, lowers the hood, drives with a quicker response, and locks its wheels in an X when you let go of the sticks.

The match, phase by phase

Our hub only counts balls while it is active. The alliance that scores more in auto has its hub closed first in the shifts.

PhaseWhat to do
Auto (20 s)Hands off. Buttons do nothing in auto. Robot: Drives its path, aims and scores.
Transition (10 s, both hubs open)Stay in our zone and score. Robot: Auto-aim is already on; fires when ready.
Our shift (25 s, our hub open)Score from inside the line. Sweep balls along our wall and behind our hub between volleys. Robot: Speed cap and X-lock while scoring.
Their shift (25 s, our hub closed)Go out through the trench, collect (vacuum), let it pass home. Come back on "Come home". Robot: Passes to our zone, holds scoring shots.
Endgame (last 30 s, both open)Full hopper, drive to the climb on "Climb now", climb, then hands off the drive sticks. Robot: Keeps shooting while hanging (operator holds RT).

Climbing
  • Driver holds D-pad Up to raise the climber and D-pad Down to pull the robot up. It stops by itself at both ends.
  • Once the climber is up, nothing automatic moves the robot or the intake.
  • While hanging, do not touch the drive sticks: the wheels spin in the air and the robot loses track of where it is.
  • The operator keeps shooting with RT. One end of the rung may put the hub in the turret's blind spot: practice which end works.
