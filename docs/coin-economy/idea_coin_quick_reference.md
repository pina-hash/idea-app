# IDEA Coin - Quick Reference Price List

Every active fine, award, and purchase, with the amount and the one-line
reason for it. Full reasoning, scoring, and audit trail live in the main
document (idea_coin_economy_draft_v3.md) - this is the distilled version
for actual day-to-day use.

---

## Fines

| Fine | Amount | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Shop Safety Violation | 12i¢ | Real physical injury risk - priced at the top on purpose. |
| Shop Not Cleaned Up | 12i¢ | Tools/materials left out are a hazard for the next student - same risk category as a safety violation. |
| Coin Theft | 12i¢ | Attacks the currency itself, not just one interaction. |
| Mint Tampering (Suspect Known) | 12i¢ | Counterfeiting risk to the whole system. |
| Mint Tampering (Suspect Unknown) | No coin fine - section mint freeze until resolved | Can't fine a student you haven't identified. |
| Classmate Trust Violation | 5i¢ | Covers framing a classmate or tampering with their computer - both a targeted betrayal of one student's trust. |
| Eating Violation | 5i¢ | Covers eating with no pass at all, or breaking pass conduct rules badly enough to strike. |
| Leaving Without Permission | 5i¢ | Real accountability and safety issue, not just an inconvenience. |
| Property Damage (Careless) | 3i¢ flat + 1i¢ per $0.25 of repair/replacement cost | Careless damage should cost what it actually costs to fix. |
| Disruptive Behavior | 3i¢ | Covers outbursts, arguing, and general disruption - one category for the same underlying thing. |
| 3D Printer Not Reset | 3i¢ | Includes the printer's flash drive going missing - the machine isn't ready either way. |
| Property Damage (Repeat Accident) | 5i¢ flat | A pattern worth a conversation, not a debt. |
| Property Damage (First Accident) | No charge, logged | Genuine accidents during proper use aren't carelessness. |
| Long Bathroom Break | 2i¢ | Minor time/honesty issue. |
| Off-Task Device Use | 2i¢ | Bypasses acceptable use. |
| Unauthorized Printing | 2i¢ | Unauthorized use of a shared resource. |
| Classroom Standards Violation | 1i¢ | Covers desktop background, seating, furniture, general tidiness, and any non-printer flash drive - one small-compliance category. |

**Debt:** balance can go negative with no cap. While negative, no purchases
of any kind until earnings bring it back to zero.

## Awards

| Award | Amount | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Highest Grade in Section (Weekly) | 1i¢/week | Matches the wage exactly - top student that week gets double pay. |
| Perfect Score on Graded Work | round(points ÷ 25)i¢, min 1i¢ | Scales with what the piece is worth in any class - 25pt quiz = 1i¢, up to 100pt test/project = 4i¢. |
| Quality Desktop Background | 3i¢ - resets monthly on a fixed date | Sustained effort, checked monthly so it isn't trivial. |
| Above and Beyond | 1-3i¢, instructor discretion | The one deliberately judgment-based category - also covers genuine peer-teaching. |
| Weekly Role Stipend - Shop Steward / Quartermaster / Safety Officer / Lab Tech (209H) | 2i¢/week while holding the role | Double the base wage - real ongoing responsibility. Stacks with wage and Highest Grade on purpose - they measure different things. |
| Donate Supplies | Small ~$1-5 = 1i¢, Medium ~$5-15 = 3i¢, Large $15+ = case-by-case | A tissue box shouldn't pay like a case of gloves. |
| Correct Answer in Class | 1i¢, once/day max | Must be correct AND actually reasoned - a lucky guess or a bare right word doesn't qualify. |
| Contract Completion | Variable - 1i¢/hour, +50% for specialized skill | Priced like a job posting. |
| Competition Winnings | Variable, case-by-case | Tournament stakes vary too widely for one number. |
| Weekly Wage | 1i¢/week, base | The floor, not the engine - guaranteed just for being enrolled. |

## Purchases

| Item | Amount | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Eating Pass | 150i¢ | A real stretch past what even your best current student has earned to date. |
| Extra Credit, per point (209H) | 2i¢/point, capped at 2% of semester points (21 pts / 42i¢ total) | Enough to move a borderline grade; not enough to substitute for actual performance. |
| Platform Cosmetic Unlock | 15-25i¢ | Universal now, not tied to one class - platform engagement, not course content. |
| Pay Raise | 40i¢ x new tier (1→2 = 40i¢, 2→3 = 80i¢...) | A permanent income increase needs a real price and gets steeper so it can't be stacked cheaply. |
| 3D Printing | Material: 1i¢/10g. Time: free under 1hr, 2i¢ (1-3hr), 4i¢ (3-6hr), 6i¢ (6hr+), free if overnight | Slicer's real numbers, not a size guess. No time charge when the print isn't actually competing with anyone else for the machine. |
| Song Request | 3i¢ | Reactivated now that requests are approval-gated (submit → your review → play), which solves the disruption problem that paused it. |
| Text Printing, per 4 pages | 1i¢ | Real cost is a few cents a page; images no longer purchasable. |
| Coin Payout | Mechanism, no price | Converts digital balance to physical coins handed over. |

## Retired (not currently active)

In-N-Out Combo, Instructor Does Your Work, Outside Time, Games During
Downtime (now a free rule), Helping Peers, Gambling, Zoned Out, Bounty
Hunting, Flash Drive Not Returned, the old Basic/Executive Eating Pass
split, four 209H unit-specific awards (Documentation Packet Submitted,
Design Revised for Manufacturing, Station Safety Sign-off, Integration
Handoff), and the separate Framing a Classmate / Tampering With a
Classmate's Computer fines (now Classmate Trust Violation). Full list
with reasons in the main document, Part 7.
