Ledger 0472, queue `docs/feedback/2026-10-28/QUEUE-0472.md`, decision 73. Mr. Pina asked for this at least four times since July (2026-07-13, 2026-08-26, decision 62, and now), and every attempt styled the launcher from a palette instead of from the apps. Ledger 0467, the day before, put the apps into six shared colour families; he called it "terrible" and "very off-brand", and asked that each card be "unique visually, completely, like real apps on a computer." Fidelity to each app was the only measure of done.

Load-bearing decisions:
- **Colours trace to files, not to a palette.** The test refuses any literal not found in the card's own `sources` and any token the room files do not read; `colors.css` alone cannot vouch for a token, because it declares every token there is. The tracer caught fourteen values on its first run (site fallbacks the home page never uses, the notebook island tokens that live only on `.nb-root`, a GREENLINE glow typed as an rgba the room never writes); each was replaced with what the room actually uses.
- **A card follows its room.** The plate rooms' cards repaint with Space White and Matrix through the tokens their rooms read; the five rooms that never change keep their cards identical in every theme, which is why IdeaCAD's green is the literal its out-of-scope room resolves to rather than `var(--green)` (Space White moves `--green` on the home page, not in IdeaCAD).
- **The words stand on the card's own ground.** `--rm-ground` is the card's background, so every contrast instrument walks to the right ground; the drawing fades in from its left edge, the title and description carry a ground-coloured halo, and the Ledger's particles moved from a full-face texture into the drawing after the pixel probe found one under the title at 4.05:1.
- **An independent review failed the first pass, and the second pass is what landed.** Six cards passed it (the Coin Ledger, GAUNTLET with one fix, GREENLINE, VANGUARD, the Foundry, FRC) and Maps passed with polish; the other eight read as one template, a charcoal field with a small picture in the top right corner. Those cards became scenes: the page itself fills the card and the title and key are laid over it. GAUNTLET's cube and triad, which were not on its run page and collided with IdeaCAD's, became the first-clear banner. The Classroom stream (with status tags the My Classes page does not show) went back to the class tiles the page does show.
- **A second review failed the second pass, on composition.** Ten cards passed; Classroom, Armory, Market and Admin still shared one chassis (a charcoal panel, icon and title top left, a row of bordered sub-tiles). Each became its page's dominant composition with its title where that page puts it: My Classes centred over two tall tiles, the Armory's file list with its name at the foot, the Market's two columns, the console's tab rail over a centred name and the identity strip. The same review asked for labelled, filled rooms in Maps with the zoom keys clear of the plan, GREENLINE's chrome wordmark with the spark burst on its streak, FRC's header strip, rule and domain cards (the ghost triangle removed), 9px as the smallest word, descriptions that end inside two lines, and a check with every word hidden. That check is now part of the capture: the grid with every word and mark hidden, and a sentence per card on what is left.
- **A third review passed, narrowly, and asked for five last things.** The Coin Ledger and VANGUARD had kept a picture at the right end of an otherwise empty field: the Ledger now draws its two stat tiles and tab keys under its name, and VANGUARD its own black ground (`#000`, the page's body; no scanline fill, which the page does not draw) with the playfield column and the pathway panel. The Armory gained its tab keys over the file list, the class titles wrap to two lines instead of losing their end, the launcher keeps 5rem under its grid so the feedback button's corner never rests on a card's key at the page's end, and the captures drop the harness's debug strip and the transient "Saving..." note.
- **GREENLINE's chrome wordmark is the one word on a card below 4.5:1 in places, and it is the room's own.** The gradient is GreenlineWordmark's recipe; its horizon band at 51% (`#39454f`, 2.06:1 on the night ground) and its foot (`#3f4b55`, 2.27:1) put about 18% of the letter height under 4.5:1, computed from the stops. `--rm-title` stays the solid `#dfe8ee` that every instrument reads. Asked for by the review; if it should not stand, removing `--rm-title-img` and `--rm-title-fill` from the GREENLINE block returns the solid ink.
- **The floating feedback button never rests on a card's key.** It sits fixed at the window's bottom right (48px tall and 72px up at most at 375; 44px and 56px up at 1440). The home harness has 847 to 1036px of page below the launcher, so every card scrolls clear of it there; and the launcher now keeps 5rem (80px) under its grid, so even where it is the last thing on a page, the last card's key scrolls above the button's corner.
- **A plate room's scene stands its tiles on plate panels over the plate's page ground**, because that is what plate.css paints (`.class-card`, `.hero-meta`, the shop tiles and `.ar-row` are all plate panels). It is also what keeps the small words legible on Space White: on the raised key face (`--plate-face-top`, `#b8bdc5` there) the class code measured 3.62:1, Open 4.09, the Admin readout 3.70 to 3.77 and Available 4.03; on the panel they all clear 4.5.
- **The Market's key moved off its balance glass**, which is now in the card's head where the page puts it; the key is the plate's own. The first version used the plate well for the key and measured 4.03:1 on Space White, and the glass ink as the mark measured 2.20:1 there; the mark is the tiles' bag-key ink and the source test holds every mark to 3:1.
- **The notebook's mark stays amber, not the NOT FILED YET chip's fill**: the chip's warn fill is a light colour meant to carry dark words, and as a mark on the light Space White card it measured 2.26:1 in the source test.
