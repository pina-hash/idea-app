**What changed.** A new standard, `IDEA_HTML_DESIGN_DIRECTIONS.md` 1.0, holds what every HTML
file built for Mr. Pina must meet: 42 universal rules drawn from his own critiques, directions
for eleven kinds of HTML, cross-cutting findings, and the persona audit run before delivery.
`IDEA_HTML_ASSIGNMENT_AUTHORING.md` goes to 1.5: three stale rules corrected (fonts, video,
theme) and a new section 13 of ten rules from Hook 02's audit.

**How it was built.** A research agent read his chats from May to September 2026 and compiled
92 critiques, 46 raised more than once, each with the quote, the chat and the date, and marked
each as captured, partial or missing against the standards in project knowledge (about 30, 33
and 29). A second agent built a research brief on eight kinds of HTML from WCAG, GOV.UK, USWDS,
NN/g and the multimedia-learning literature, marking every figure it could not open. His rulings
are kept separate from research defaults in the new file, and his ruling wins where they
disagree (the percent progress bar and following the site's theme are the two places they do).

**Not verified.** The research brief's quotes came through a summarizing fetch tool; the new
file says to confirm wording at the source before quoting a number elsewhere. The Rotation 2
chat of 2026-09-25 to 09-29 did not come up in chat search, so its critiques come from the
authoring changelog and the session's own state file.

**Left open.** `IDEA_instructions.md` still calls standalone HTML "legacy", and
`IDEA_DS_DIGEST.md` still lists grid backgrounds; both are named in the new file.
