Mr. Pina's brief: every chat starts current, applies the right rules, and produces work that looks like IDEA today. An audit of the instructions field (9,894 words), 51 project knowledge docs, 24 standards files and the saved skills found the rules buried and duplicated (77 duplicated rules across the set), three project copies behind the mirror, routing entries pointing at files project knowledge did not hold, and the look prescribed by four sources that still described the July machined palette (`//`, blueprint grids, neon greens) while the site had moved to the Plate and Space White. A replay of ten real past requests confirmed it: stale facts were the most common failure, then length, then a generic or out-of-date look.

What changed here:

- `docs/project-instructions.md`: the source of the instructions field, about 700 words of always-true rules.
- `docs/skills/`: the source of every IDEA skill. Three were already saved (idea-state-of-world, idea-closeout, deeper-not-bigger); six are new (idea-code-prompt, idea-design, idea-course-material, my-writing-style, deck-humor, uc-course-description). A skill is live only once Mr. Pina saves its card.
- `docs/KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md`: the map of which layer holds what.
- `IDEA_instructions.md` 4.34: the rationale record behind the field and skills, plus "Operate as the project lead, not the courier", and 5.0 model names read as their 5.5 successors.
- `IDEA_CLAUDE_DESIGN_STANDARDS.md` 2.2: its visual identity section is marked superseded by the IDEA Design System artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/Gb9XpTcK43gZMGSkqy22xs).
- `IDEA_PRINT_STANDARDS.md` 1.2: print type and color from the system's space-white theme.
- `docs/archive/project-knowledge/`: verbatim copies of three retired project knowledge docs that had no other home here.

For a person to act on: paste `docs/project-instructions.md` (below its rule) into the project's instructions field, save the skill cards, and set the IDEA Design System as the default design system.

Before-and-after proof, 2026-10-17: ten of Mr. Pina's real past requests were replayed with the old setup and the new one and scored by a blind judge. The new setup was preferred 5 times, the old 3, with 2 ties. Look scored 4 against 2 on both visual requests (the old side brought back the // motif, a grid and gold and copper accents). Skill triggering was exact on 18 of 20 test prompts. Three gaps the proof found are fixed in this follow-up: the field now says nothing is reported as done until it is read back (`docs/project-instructions.md` 1.1), `IDEA_context.md` 2.3 says which pacing workbooks exist and that a missing one is asked for, and the idea-design skill says a survey or form is built in the app, never a Google Form.
