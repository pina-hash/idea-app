The fourth lane of the site-wide visual pass (feedback 38516c68 and c27b2be7, queue `docs/feedback/2026-10-28/QUEUE-site-ui-pass.md`, section "Lane 0468"), built on 0465's tokens, 0466's StatusChip and 0467's identity colours.

What changed:
- ONE PLATE SOURCE WITH THE KIT. `tools/hx-kit/kit/plate.css` (the HTML kit's part classes) is now loaded by the site too: `src/lib/shell/site-plate.css` imports it, and `src/lib/classroom/plate.css` gained section 1b, which declares every name that file reads (`--hair`, `--key-face-top`, the thirteen `--shadow-*`, ...) from the Plate's own tokens. The names are declared on the PARTS (`:where(.cr-plate, .site-plate) :where(.idea-key, ...)`), never on the plate root, so a room that re-points `--plate-accent` on one element reaches a part inside it; `--radius-control` (the site's 3px, the kit's 11px) is declared only on `idea-key` and `idea-well`. No part recipe is restated in `src/`.
- THE KIT FILE'S CHANGE (tools/hx-kit/ belongs to the HTML standards chat; this lane may edit only `kit/plate.css`): a header paragraph saying it is the one source for both; three new parts the site needed, `idea-pad` and `idea-pads` (the classroom's section-tab pad with its LED, lit by `aria-current`), `idea-list` (the list well with etched rows) and `idea-display` (a small readout on the display glass, `idea-display-label` for its words); and `idea-titlebar`, `idea-pad` and `idea-eyebrow` written with their class twice, because rooms style every `h1` and `a` inside them at (0,1,1) (`.nb-root h1`, `.fg-root a`) and turned a single-class title bar into the room's own heading and the pads' words cyan. Every token the new parts read already exists in `tokens.json`. `starter.html` was not rebuilt (it is the HTML chat's); `node tools/hx-kit/test.mjs tools/hx-kit/starter.html` and the same test over a scratch rebuild of the starter from the changed kit both read 0 FAIL, 1 WARN (steel and magenta under deuteranopia, which was there before), 50 PASS.
- `src/lib/shell/PageHeader.svelte`: the cyan eyebrow, the title bar and one meta line, actions on their own centred row. A `div`, not a `header` (`.legacy-index header` is a sticky flexed masthead rule). Mounted by the coin home, market, stocks, a stock, My coins, My class role, the coin staff layout, the Foundry page, build contract, publisher page and publish page, the Maps viewer and editor, the certifications shell, the admin console and seven admin pages plus the feedback connector page, the calendar, the notebook head and the notebook timeline.
- `src/lib/shell/AppSubNav.svelte`: the pads strip (links, or buttons with `onselect` for a harness), a lit count with a warning-solid lamp, an optional blurb. Mounted as the coin pages' nav (`src/lib/coin-bar/CoinPagesNav.svelte`), the coin desk's areas (`CoinDeskNav`), the Foundry's places (moved under the masthead's logo row), the certifications' four places and the desk's tabs (moved out of the header), the Maps editor (Public map, Editor, Shelf entry) and the admin area (`src/lib/shell/AdminSubNav.svelte`, `ADMIN_PLACES`).
- THE LEDGER IS UNTOUCHED. `CoinBar` is server-rendered into the frozen Coin Ledger, so it was left exactly as it was and the site's coin pages mount `CoinPagesNav` over the same page list instead; `tests/fixtures/coin-bar-ledger-golden.json`, captured from the tree before this lane, holds `ledgerCoinBarHtml` byte for byte. The feedback pill's fold (below) lives in `SiteFeedback`, never in `FeedbackTrigger`, which the Ledger also serves.
- Cards, lists, readouts and keys: balances in a page header are `idea-display`; the coin home's rows, the market's trade lists (nine lists) and My coins' things to buy are `idea-list` rows rather than boxes in a card; the coin home's stat strip, held pay and the market's banner candidates lost their nested boxes; the admin console's FRC model and GREENLINE decal queues are list wells with etched rows (`plate.css`), not cards in a card. 48 left stripes (2px to 5px `border-left` in a tone or identity colour) were removed across the coin pages, the coin desk, certifications, the Foundry, Maps, the calendar, the notebook, the Live grid and two admin pages; each of those surfaces already says its tone in a word, a glyph or a StatusChip. Foundry's Launch full screen, Copy link and Open review controls became plain keys, leaving Launch app the one green key on that card; the Foundry inspector's fifteen default keys became plain keys beside its one send key; the Foundry review area's Apps, Publishers and Curation (three green keys) are pads (`FoundryReviewNav` on `AppSubNav`). The coin home's rank numbers take the well's own ink (gold read 4.06:1 there on Space White) and the display label lost its 85% opacity (3.84:1).
- c27b2be7: the signed-out landing no longer drops a visitor into the dimming spotlight; `HomeTourOffer` shows the classroom's inline "Show me around" row (recorded in `TOUR_SEEN_KEY` when it shows, so once per browser) and the tour runs only when asked (`HomeTour` auto-launches only a signed-in first run).
