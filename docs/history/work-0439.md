### Load-bearing decisions

- **One rule, email-keyed, with wrappers** (the 0138 shape), because the phone link has no
  session. Minting a session per calendar-app poll (the connector's pattern) was rejected: a
  phone polls hourly per subscriber, and it would put a ninth reader on the service key.
- **A class is the viewer's when enrolled or teacher of record, never "manages"**: an admin
  manages every section, and his calendar is not the whole school.
- **The school calendar is read server-side and never stored.** Its public address answered
  200 (3.8 MB, 10,882 events since 2020) on 2026-10-26, so it is the default and the variable
  is optional. The parse is cached per instance for 30 minutes, served stale for a day.
- **Robotics**: the Techmen app already publishes a per-member iCalendar feed
  (`supabase/functions/calendar-feed` in frc-app, `?token=&scope=all`); its address goes in
  `ROBOTICS_CALENDAR_ICS_URL`. Admin-added robotics events here work with or without it.
- **Pacing is a display key with one database resolver.** `day` counts school days from the
  day the post opens, so one document paces every section and year; `date` is a fixed day.
  Admin moves are stored beside the post (not per section: a post's due date is per post too,
  and the posting carries no state).
- **The calendar is not a class tab**; the strip is its door (six tabs already on a manager's
  bar at 375px). The strip sits under the class header (inside the `classTeams` snippet) and
  under the My Classes title.
- **Weekday column heads** (Su to Sa) appear on the month grid only; no date anywhere is
  written with a weekday name.
