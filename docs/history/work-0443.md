Mr. Pina asked that students "just take their phone out and take the picture within the HTML". The Unit 4 authoring chat wrote `tools/hx-kit/kit/camera.js` and left it as a patch (its container cannot push); the IDEA & FRC chat applied it unchanged and added the test gate, the standard and the skill change in one push (ledger 0443).

What changed:
- `kit/camera.js` (inlined after `team.js`): on a touch screen (`pointer: coarse`) every picture box's Choose key gets a "Take photo" twin that sets `capture="environment"` on the box's own file input for that one press, so the camera opens directly; Choose still opens the library. A picture over 1.5 MB is redrawn at a 2400 px longest side and sent as JPEG 0.85 (a capture-phase `change` listener runs before the bridge's own and hands the smaller file to `hxAttach`). Not a picture, already small, or a format the browser cannot draw: sent untouched. On a computer the key is hidden by `kit.css`.
- `test.mjs` section 6, run when a document has a picture box: in an emulated phone (390x844, touch, mobile) every picture box with a Choose key shows Take photo; at 1440 with a mouse none shows; tapping it opens the file chooser with `capture=environment`; a JPEG of at least 10 MB made in the browser reaches the portal under 2 MB. `host.html` records the decoded size of every uploaded picture so the gate can read it. `openHost` takes context options.
- `IDEA_HTML_ASSIGNMENT_AUTHORING.md` 2.7: F10 phone camera; A4 a rules check never gives its answer away in the question; A6 and Part H gate 5 worked examples recomputed by arithmetic; G5 a timed research step with real links before sketching, and a team post explains the team document; F14 thumbnail and banner text from `--idea-thumb-h`, never `vh`; D3 avoid hues 40 to 105 for a main hue (brown in the light theme); Part H gate 4 the cold open plays three named 10th graders and the Block 4 teacher.
- `docs/skills/idea-html-test/SKILL.md`: the cold open plays Maya (strong), Jordan (average), Theo (missed the first day) and the Block 4 teacher with no Claude access, each reporting where they would get stuck or guess; the practicality pass recomputes worked examples.
