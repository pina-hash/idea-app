**What 0316 does.** One rule, `_classroom_team_file_visible(file, email)`: a picture in a `team` or `role` block, read by a member of the uploader's team in that section's current draw. **The page.** `TeamDocSession` reads `classroom_team_files` with the document (`teamImagesFrom`), and `ItemDetail`'s one image map `htmlImages` feeds the frame, the progress rail and the pacing strip. After this page adds, removes or captions a picture in a team box the session reads the team's pictures again.

**Decision: an individual block's picture stays its uploader's.** It is that student's own graded answer (the concept sketch in Launcher: Design is one), and showing it to teammates would show one student's work to another. Spec 16.3 now says so (1.14).

**The camera (ed0b584e).** The worksheet iframe carried a sandbox and no `allow`. ## Part 3: table rows, held names, team tools (migration 0324)

Queue items 8, 30 and 29.2 to 29.5.

**Found.** A team `table` block was one box with one lock, so the 35-cell u4d-wot locked for the whole team (gap 2d621bcc); the db test records it on the chain short of 0324. Separate boxes never shared a lock: the lock is keyed by box in 0302 and the kit's focusout already treats a move inside one table as staying in the box. Nothing checked a team name against the rosters (3d4d8b76).

**Built.** 0324: a row lock key `<block>#r<n>`; `classroom_team_box_lock` never locks a whole table; `_classroom_save_team_response` merges a table row by row under each changed row's lock and answers `kept_rows`. The kit names the row on focus and blur, shuts only held rows (`rows` on a table's team-state) and lays teammates' saved rows down around the one being typed in (`teamAdoptRows`); `HtmlAssignmentFrame`, the bridge parse and `TeamDocSession` carry `row`. `_idea_roster_name_hits` is the shared roster-name check; the `classroom_teams_name_hold` trigger holds a student's name or tagline that matches a classmate in `classroom_team_text_holds`, decided on the sign-offs page (`TeamNameHolds`). `classroom_team_box_history_mine` and `TeamBoxHistory` under a team post; `classroom_machine_week` and `/classroom/board/machines`; `classroom_item_section_shift_set` (the teams panel's "runs N school days late") moves the class's pacing steps (`classroom_item_pacing`, the caller's own class) and its team checkpoint dates and phase dates; `TeamTodayCard` on the class home.

**Decisions.** A held name is not applied at all (the old name stays) rather than shown with a flag: Mr. Pina's report asks for "held for his OK before it shows". The roster check matches whole words of four letters or more against every active student's given and family names, the team's own members excepted; false positives cost one tap on the sign-offs page. Shifts move later only (0 to 30 school days), through 0308's `_calendar_school_day_n`, never a second school-day rule; seeded task dates already stored do not move with a shift (a team edits its own dates).

**For other lanes.** Tournament entry text (lane 0455's files) and market listings should call `_idea_roster_name_hits(text, except_emails)` and hold on a hit; nothing here touches them.
