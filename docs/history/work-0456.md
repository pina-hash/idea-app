**What was wrong, measured.** A picture is a `classroom_submission_files` row on the uploader's own submission, and its three readers (0086's row policy, 0133's storage predicate `classroom_can_read_submission_object`, the engine read on the item page) answered only the uploader and the teacher. `tests/db/classroom-team-photos.test.ts` records it on the chain short of 0316: a teammate read 0 rows and 0 objects of a role picture its owner uploaded through the real RPC. The progress payload (`classroom_team_progress`) counted a member's own rows only, so a role box read empty for everyone but its owner and the team's percent differed by student (aac90533). `_classroom_first_name` took the first word of "Last, First", so a teammate read "Chavarria,".

**What 0316 does.** One rule, `_classroom_team_file_visible(file, email)`: a picture in a `team` or `role` block, read by a member of the uploader's team in that section's current draw. A second permissive row policy asks it through a caller-scoped wrapper, and `classroom_can_read_submission_object` gains one branch asking it, so the file route's signed URL is minted on the teammate's own session. `classroom_team_files(item)` lists the team's newest picture per box; `classroom_add_submission_file` (0197's body, one branch) refuses a role picture from anyone but the role's owner through 0302's `_classroom_team_block_refusal`; `classroom_team_progress` counts the team's own answers and pictures for every member. Names: `_classroom_given_name` reads "Last, First" and "First Last"; `_classroom_team_display_name` gives "First L." where two members of one team share a first name; the team document names members through it.

**The page.** `TeamDocSession` reads `classroom_team_files` with the document (`teamImagesFrom`), and `ItemDetail`'s one image map `htmlImages` feeds the frame, the progress rail and the pacing strip. After this page adds, removes or captions a picture in a team box the session reads the team's pictures again.

**Decision: an individual block's picture stays its uploader's.** It is that student's own graded answer (the concept sketch in Launcher: Design is one), and showing it to teammates would show one student's work to another. Spec 16.3 now says so (1.14).

**The camera (ed0b584e).** The worksheet iframe carried a sandbox and no `allow`. The kit's Take photo is a file input with `capture` (`tools/hx-kit/kit/camera.js`, unchanged), which a phone opens from inside the frame; the frame is cross-origin with an opaque origin and the camera's Permissions-Policy default allowlist is `self`, so the capture view got no camera. `HX_FRAME_ALLOW = 'camera'` is now on the frame, and nothing else powerful; `tests/html-assignment-frame-permissions.test.ts` holds the grant and the refusals. The cause was on the site side; nothing in `tools/hx-kit` was changed.

**Built.** 0323: `classroom_team_post_draws` pins a post per class; a newer draw moves it only while `_classroom_team_post_used` is false; `_classroom_team_ctx`, the file rule, progress, the team items, the grading gate and `classroom_item_teams` read the pin (students: while posted, end time ignored; instructor and grading: always). Every stored team post was pinned to the draw it used at apply. `classroom_team_set_uses` feeds the People tab's warning before Take down and Retire. `_classroom_signoff_missing` over `_classroom_team_block_done` names who is missing what and is now the field check itself; `classroom_signoff_teacher_decide` decides from the post, opening a request if none, and approves over a failure only with a note; `classroom_signoff_waiting` counts for the header door and the home line; `classroom_item_signoff_board` is the teams panel. `_classroom_team_seed_due` dates seeded tasks (stored undated ones are filled on the next read). `_classroom_team_balance` uses each member's own share, with `rule` "roles" on a small team; the plan prints how to even it out. `opensAfter` (spec 16.10) holds a module shut per team until its checkpoint is approved or dated. Page: `SignoffCard` (one checklist for the queue and the panel), `TeamSignoffPanel` on the post, the Sign-offs door with its count, the home line, `TeamGradeWork` (the document in the real frame, team boxes plus a picked member's own, who wrote each box) beside `TeamGrader`, which now takes a typed score with a note between levels; the tour offer is never shown to staff (cd54d2ee).

**Decision: a used post never moves by itself.** "Used" is a team answer, a team picture, a sign-off request or a grade on the old teams; an untouched post follows a newer draw, so a post made a minute before the next rotation's draw still lands on the new teams. A pin can be moved only by retiring its draw while unused work exists, which the People tab warns about.
