# 68 Coin staff have two tiers: full coin admins and section staff

- Raised: 2026-10-15  By: Mr. Pina, in the IDEA Coin V2 session two prompt (ledger 0386), on what Mr. Cosso may do in the coin system.
- Status: DECIDED 2026-10-15 by Mr. Pina, in the prompt: the owner and anyone he lists hold full coin powers; every other site admin, Mr. Cosso included, and every class's teacher of record work the coin system for their own sections only.
- Decision: Two tiers of coin staff, both enforced in the database.
  - **Full coin admin**: the owner (`admin_owner_email()`), plus each address on the list at `/coin-desk/settings` (`coin_full_admins`, which only a full coin admin edits and which takes only an `@boscotech.edu` address; a listed address that is not also a site admin gets nothing, because `_coin_full_staff()` asks `is_admin()` first). Everything in the coin system: settings and prices, editing a past amount, reversing autopay, the Log for any student, contracts, roles, the economy list, tournament transfers, and every section.
  - **Section coin staff**: every other site admin (Mr. Cosso is one) and every class's teacher of record (`classroom_sections.teacher_email`). For the students enrolled in their own sections only: the fine screen, Award, the queue, payday, the digest and each student's stats page and export. No settings, no amount edits, no autopay reversals, no other section's students. Section scope is read from `teacher_email`, not from `classroom_manages_section`, because that function answers yes for every section to any site admin.
  - Nothing about any other admin power changed. Being a site admin still gates everything it gated before; this decision only narrows what an admin may do inside the coin system.
- Built as: `supabase/migrations/0244_coin_v2_staff_scope.sql` (`_coin_full_staff`, `_coin_staff`, `_coin_section_in_scope`, `_coin_student_in_scope`, `coin_staff_scope`, `coin_full_admin_set`, and a sweep that moved every coin function that opened with `is_admin()` onto `_coin_full_staff()`; 0251 runs the sweep again over everything session two added). The Coin Desk reads `coin_staff_scope()` once in its layout (`src/lib/server/coin-scope.ts`) and offers only the areas a tier may open (`areasForScope` in `src/lib/coin-desk/nav.ts`); each area's page re-checks with `requireCoinArea`, and an area a tier may not open answers 404.
- Supersedes: the session-one rule that every coin write opens with `is_admin()` (CLAUDE.md "EVERY ADMIN COIN WRITE OPENS WITH `is_admin()`"), which is now `_coin_full_staff()` for full powers and `_coin_staff()` plus a scope check for section powers.
- Context: `docs/coin-economy/IDEA_COIN_V2.md` (staff scope); `tests/db/coin-v2-session-two.test.ts` asserts both tiers in both directions, with a mutation proof.
