# 70 IDEA Coin later features: marketplace, reviews, transfers, header banner, hall pass time, stock market

- Raised: 2026-10-17  By: Mr. Pina, in the IDEA Coin reform chat, choosing which of the later features listed in `docs/coin-economy/IDEA_COIN_V2.md` to build.
- Status: DECIDED 2026-10-17 by Mr. Pina: "build out the student marketplace, marketplace reviews, student to student transfers, header banner, buy hall pass time, and the stock market."
- Decision: All six, with the defaults below, every number editable on `/coin-desk/settings` and audited.
  - **Marketplace** (`/market`): an Official zone (staff only: the shop, donation want-ads, the desktop background, staff cards, contracts, restitution jobs for a student in debt) and a Student zone (offers and requests; title, description, a price with two decimals, an optional photo, a category, a quantity or one time; at most 5 open listings; 14 days, renewable). Every trade is escrowed: a claim holds the coins, the seller marks it done, the buyer confirms and the coins move less a sale fee (default 0%); an unconfirmed, undisputed sale pays itself after 5 class days; the seller may cancel before delivery for a full refund; either side may dispute and staff release, refund or split. Staff feature or hide a listing with a reason.
  - **Reviews**: one per side after a trade that paid out, 1 to 5 stars, at least 20 characters, a profile shows the average, the count and the 10 newest, no edit after 24 hours, none on a refunded trade, staff remove one with a reason.
  - **Transfers**: a 4 to 6 digit PIN, hashed; 5 wrong tries lock sending for 15 minutes; any student in the economy to any other; a message up to 200 characters; at most 20 coins a school day; never while in debt; public by default on the Ledger and the market; private costs 10% (at least 0.1), a sink. **The private fee is to be tuned from how students use it**: the defaults are a starting point, not a finding.
  - **Classroom header banner**: staff pins with optional dates and an optional class, rotating, a click opens one, Hide for today; motion stops on request and under reduced motion; works on Space White. Three looks were built (Spotlight, Ticker, Cards) and **Spotlight is the default** until Mr. Pina picks on `/coin-desk/settings`.
  - **Buy hall pass time**: 5-minute blocks at 1 coin, at most 15 extra minutes a day. Bought time adds to today's budget and moves the 10-minute warning and the 20-minute fine later by the same amount, for passes opened after the purchase only. **The fine threshold never moves past the auto check-in cap, and a fine already given is never removed.**
  - **Stock market** (`/market/stocks`): prices made from recorded site data with every close storing its inputs and formula version; class stocks (grade ratio, trailing 4 weeks), FNDRY (distinct Foundry people a day, 7-day average), GNTLT (distinct GAUNTLET submitters a day, 7-day average) and the IDEA index; price = 10 x (signal / 8-week median), at most 10% a day, between 1 and 100, nightly on class days; trades with the house at the last close, fractional shares, 2% fee each way, 50-coin cost-basis cap per stock, class days only, no shorting, selling always allowed; a portfolio is never part of a balance.
  - **Economy health** on the digest: circulation, the week's sinks against its faucets, escrow and stock totals.
- Built as: ledger 0402, migrations 0275 (the `market` short link reserved) through 0281. `docs/coin-economy/IDEA_COIN_V2.md` section 12 carries the defaults table and every rule; the history entry `docs/history/` for that ledger carries the backtest, the banner candidates and the calls made.
- Open for Mr. Pina: pick the banner look (`/coin-desk/settings`, "Classroom banner look"), and later, tune the private transfer fee once there is a month of use.
