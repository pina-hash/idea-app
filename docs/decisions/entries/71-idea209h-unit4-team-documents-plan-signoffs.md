# 71 IDEA209H Unit 4: one shared document per team, a team plan that drives a tracker, a sign-off queue

- Raised: 2026-10-23  By: Mr. Pina, in the IDEA & FRC chat, for the IDEA209H Unit 4 team launcher project (Oct 26 to Nov 16; Block 2 has 10 students, Block 3 has 19, Block 4 has 11; Mr. Cosso teaches Block 4 with no Claude access, so nothing may need an unwritten judgment call).
- Status: DECIDED 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-26 by Mr. Pina, confirmed in chat, with two addenda on 2026-10-26 (posting a draw is the switch, and the power source; teams managed through the connector). Not to be reopened.
- Decision:
  - **The shared team document.** An HTML assignment can be a team assignment; team sizes are 3 or 4. Team modules hold ONE answer per block per team, editable by every member, graded once. Owned modules are keyed to a subsystem ROLE declared by the assignment (Unit 4: frame and mount, energy store, drive, ball path), never hardcoded; the role's owner comes from the team plan; only the owner edits an owned block, teammates view it live and read only, enforced on the server. On a team of 3 one student owns two roles and gets two owned blocks. Per-box lock, not character merging: focusing a box takes its lock and shows "<first name> is editing"; the lock releases on blur or after 2 minutes idle; the server refuses a save from anyone but the lock holder. Saves autosave, debounced; teammates see a saved change within about 2 seconds through a payload-free notice plus a re-read; answer content never goes over a socket. Every save writes a change-log row (who, when, block, before, after), and instructors open any box and see its full history. After a post's due date its fields are read only. A student sees only their own team's document; instructors see every team in sections they manage; admins see all.
  - **Posting a draw is the switch (addendum 1, 2026-10-26).** The Unit 4 team posts go up before teams exist, as one post shared by all three sections. Each section resolves its own team set at read time: that section's current posted team set. Never bind at upload; never require a re-upload or a manual link after a draw. Nothing changes for students until he taps Post; the moment Post returns, every student in that section with the post open lands in their own team's document within 2 seconds, with no reload. Unposting and a hand edit to a posted draw (decision 44) take effect the same way. Before a set is posted a student sees exactly: "Teams are drawn in class. Your team's document opens here the moment teams are posted." A student moved to another team keeps nothing of the old team's document: answers stay with the team and role block they were written in, and the change log records the move.
  - **The team plan and tracker.** One plan per team per unit. A task has a task, owner, due date, linked field (assignment and block), points, optional machine tag (laser, router, printer, mill, lathe; the list is admin-editable data) and status. Every graded block of the team's assignments arrives as a task with its rubric points and linked field fixed; owned tasks are pre-assigned to the role owner and the team assigns owners for team tasks only; teams may add their own tasks at 0 points, which count toward Behind and never toward share. Roles are assigned in the plan; the plan cannot be submitted until every role has an owner and each student holds 0.75x to 1.25x an even share of total points. Status: Done is automatic (the linked field passes the existing completion rule; no done button, no turn-in button); On it comes from a start-of-class check-in where each student taps the tasks they are on today; Blocked is the only status a student sets, with a one-line reason; Behind is past due and not done; Slipping is done points below 80% of planned points to date. Sign-off 1 approves the plan, after which owners are fixed; due dates may move, never past the post's due date, every move logged; an absent student keeps their tasks and their dates may move, logged. The student view is a timeline (one row per student, tasks as blocks across school days to Nov 16, sign-off dates as vertical lines, a today line, an optional "needs task X first" link); school days skip weekends and admin-editable no-school dates, seeded with Nov 11. The board view (instructors) is per team: a burn-up chart of planned against done points by school day with each student's stacked share, percent done, Behind count, Slipping and Uneven flags, next sign-off status, and a per-machine count of tagged tasks by day across all three sections. The tracker also renders inside the HTML document through a bridge message, following the `idea:team-progress` pattern.
  - **The sign-off queue.** Four checkpoints per team: Oct 28 (Plan), Nov 2 (Design), Nov 9 (Safe to test), Nov 12 (Competition ready). Checklists are admin-editable data seeded as Mr. Pina wrote them; "auto" items are checked by the site and "tap" items are pass or fail by the instructor. Any team member taps Request sign-off, which unlocks only when that checkpoint's auto items pass. The queue is on his phone, oldest first: per request the team, the checklist, a tap on each item, then Approve or Send back, under 30 seconds per team at 375px. Approve stamps the instructor's name and time into the change log and the team sees it live. Send back makes the failed items the note with no typing; each failed item becomes a 0-point task for its role owner (or the team, for a team item); the team may request again at any time. Admins approve any section; a section manager approves only their own; an item marked owner-only routes the request to Mr. Pina alone. The board shows every team in every section to admins and their own sections to managers. Sign-offs carry no points; they gate the shop: no metal is cut before sign-off 2 and no firing happens before sign-off 3, shown plainly on the board. Build on the 0086 approval gate's concept and tables if it fits; never a second approval system. Push notification on a new request only if the existing push path makes it small.
  - **The seed checklists**, with the power-source change of addendum 1 (2026-10-26: no shared power unit, no dual CIM, no hex bore coupler; launchers are powered by the shop's 3-speed DeWalt drills by default, the DeWalt impact driver, other shop power tools or a team-built power source are allowed, and a gear ratio is optional): Plan: auto, every task has an owner and a due date; every role has an owner; the plan balances; each role's requirement and sketch fields are complete; tap, the sketches meet at the named interfaces; tap, owner only, the launch method is a flywheel or a catapult. Design: auto, the Design post's fields are complete, including each role's prototype field, and its calculators have been run; tap, the structure is aluminum with wood only for a minor part; tap, a process is named for every part; tap, the team uses 6 or fewer 1/2 in hex bearings; tap, the drill or power tool is clamped or mounted to the launcher, never hand-held while firing. Safe to test: tap, frame rigid; fasteners tight; rotating parts guarded; power stops the moment the trigger or switch is released. Competition ready: auto, the test procedure field is complete; tap, three test shots fired in an approved area.
  - **Grading.** Team or owned is read per module from the manifest; there is no criterion-level tag. The rubric is still generated from the manifest at upload. The console grades BY TEAM, one screen per team per post: team criteria are scored once and copied to every member through the existing bulk path, with no per-student override on team criteria; owned criteria are scored per role block, side by side; a double owner's owned score is the mean of their two blocks, so every student's maximum stays 175. Design Review is a live team presentation, scored in the room on a phone at 375px. `IDEA_RUBRIC_STANDARDS.md` 1.4 records team modules, role-owned modules, the double-owner mean, no override on team criteria, and sign-off checklists kept out of rubrics.
  - **The connector (addendum 2, 2026-10-26).** Teams are fully manageable from the IDEA Website connector, by voice, on the same server functions and audit trail as the site's buttons: draw teams for a class on the server (team size 3 or 4, never 2 or 5, an optional list of students to leave out, the seed kept; the teams read back before anything is saved, then saved and optionally posted in one step), swap two students between teams, add a team, remove an empty team, rename a team; assign or change a role owner (after sign-off 1 the change asks first and is logged); read one team's plan and tracker; move a task's due date (logged, never past the post's due date); read the sign-off queue, approve a request, send it back with the named failed items. Levels: reads are read; draw, post, rename, add team and swap are undo; remove team, owner changes after sign-off 1, approve and send back are confirm.
