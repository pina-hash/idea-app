# 58 The hall pass is a time budget, and the whole room sees who is out

- Raised: 2026-10-14  By: feedback round 2026-10-14 (ledger 0380), report 7348d14c.
- Status: DECIDED 2026-10-14 by Mr. Pina's own spec; the gaps are this round's defaults and each is one line to change.
- Decision: Each student gets **10 minutes of pass time per school day** per class (the instructor can change it per section). Time over the day is debt that comes off the next day's budget (15 minutes on day one leaves 5 on day two) and **all debt clears Monday 00:00 America/Los_Angeles**. A pass open longer than **30 minutes** (instructor-adjustable per section) is **checked in automatically at 30 and charged the full 30**. A student whose debt exceeds what is left in the week cannot take a pass until the reset, and is told the day. **Everyone in the class sees who is currently checked out, at all times**, by their shown name and the time they left, never an address. This replaces 0174's per-day count and cooldown; both are removed, not kept beside it.
- Defaults chosen where the spec was silent: the budget is per class (the pass was already per section); the budget starts at the instant 0237 applies, so no earlier pass is charged retroactively; the auto check in happens lazily on the next read of the class's pass (nothing runs on a timer) and records the close at the cap, not at the read; an instructor sending a student out by hand ignores the student's budget and is charged to it like any other pass; a day is the Los Angeles calendar day.
- Reverses: 0143's rule that a student is told one bit (taken or free) and never who. Mr. Pina wants the room able to notice a student who forgot to check back in.
