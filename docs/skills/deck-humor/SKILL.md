# Deck humor pass

Source of truth: `docs/skills/deck-humor/SKILL.md` in pina-hash/idea-app; the full calibrated standard is `docs/standards/IDEA_HUMOR_STANDARDS.md` and the cooldown record is `IDEA_HUMOR_LEDGER.md` (project knowledge, mirrored in the repo). Read the ledger before the pass and write it at delivery. Audience: Bosco Tech grades 9-12; presenter Mr. Pina.

## Three gates, all three or it is not proposed

1. **An argument, not a mood**: comparison, dilemma, escalation, sequence, reveal, ranking, refusal. Never a single image conveying a feeling. Multi-panel formats must differ visually panel to panel.
2. **Photographic, polished animation or high-production render.** Never the crude web-drawing family (wojak, soyjak, MS Paint line art) unless re-rendered in-house.
3. **Source and register**: no current slang he would have to say (6-7, aura, mogging, rizz, skibidi, Ohio), no anime stills, no The Office, name the source so he can strike it.

## Currency

Web articles about a meme mean it is dead. Use, in order: what he has heard quoted in the shop (the ledger's Overheard log), the nostalgia canon (about 2001-2016), and a live test in one section first.

## The payload is local

A real mistake this class made, a part that failed, a phrase said in the shop, the step everybody skips, his own wrong estimate. A row whose payload could be any school's is unfinished. No local payload available means a zero row, not invented color.

## Rated formats

Comparison: Drake (excellent), Distracted boyfriend, Buff Doge vs Cheems (risky). Dilemma: Two buttons, Trolley problem. Escalation: Expanding brain (strongest), Spy Kids zoom, Stonks. Sequence: Gru's plan, 3000 years later (filler). Reveal: Scooby Doo mask. Ranking: Tier list. Refusal: Uno draw 25, Gigachad. In-house: deep fry of the deck's own image, rubric ambush, fake comment section on the class's own render (comments specific and technically literate, never slang), absurdist full-spec treatment of a wrong object. Every sourced row needs an exact asset query and an in-house fallback.

## Placement

Never two in a row; at least two clean sheets between; no structure repeated in a deck, no format repeated in a unit; never on a bookend or a slide with a click target; prefer the secondary panel; deletable without breaking the slide's argument. Humor may harden a consequence, never soften one. Never weapons, violence or implied harm. Never aimed at a student. A named adult only if he authored or approved it. Sourced imagery never leaves the building: anything exported, sent home or public uses in-house fallbacks.

## Output

One table for him to strike rows from: Slide | Structure | Format | Source | Local payload | Exact copy | Asset query | Fallback. Cooldown-blocked rows shown struck through with the blocking ledger row. Then log every insertion, including cut ones, to the ledger in the same pass as delivery.
