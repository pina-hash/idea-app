# IDEA Morning Announcements Standard
**Version 1.0 - 2026-09-30**

How FRC Team 5669 and the IDEA pathway get an item into Bosco Tech's morning
announcements: where the request goes, when it is due, what the email must carry, how the
announcement itself is written, and what Claude does when asked for one. FRC is the main
user and has its own section. Voice rules live in `Writing_Voice_Guide.md`; this file
does not restate them, it applies them to one channel.

**Source.** The office rules in "Submission" are from Cathy Rivera's email "Morning
Announcements", sent to all students, faculty-staff and coaches on 2026-09-30 at 10:43 AM.
Where this file derives something from that email rather than quoting its substance, the
rule says so. Everything outside "Submission" is this assistant's default, standing
until Mr. Pina changes it. Where the office changes a rule, the office wins and this file is updated in
the same turn.

## Submission (office rules)

1. **To:** Cathy Rivera, `crivera@boscotech.edu`.
   **Cc, every time:** Miguel Ramirez `mramirez@boscotech.edu` and Gabriella Holguin
   `gholguin@boscotech.edu`. On days Mrs. Rivera is out, Ms. Holguin prepares and sends
   the announcements, which is why the cc is never optional.
2. **Never send a request to the shared announcement email address.** The office said
   requests sent there can be missed.
3. **Due 2:00 PM the day before it airs.** A revision to an announcement already
   submitted has the same deadline.
4. **Weekend rule.** A request received over the weekend airs Tuesday morning at the
   earliest. Derived from that: **to air on a Monday, the request is in by 2:00 PM the
   Friday before.** Treat the deadline as 2:00 PM on the last school day before the air
   date, and check `calendar_2026-09-01_2027-03-01.pdf` for a holiday or non-school day
   in between.
5. **The message goes in the body of the email**, and the email states the date, time,
   location and person to contact for the thing being announced.
6. **No flyer unless the flyer carries more than the message.** A flyer that only repeats
   the text is refused. In place of a flyer, attach one colorful image.

## The request email

One email per announcement. Every request has this shape, with nothing before it:

```
Subject: Announcement request - air <Month D> - FRC Team 5669 <topic>

Please announce on: <each date, Month D, YYYY>

ANNOUNCEMENT
<the text exactly as it should be read or printed>

DETAILS
Date: <Month D, YYYY>
Time: <start - end>
Location: <room or place>
Contact: Mr. Pina, apina@boscotech.edu

- Mr. Pina
```

- **The subject leads with the air date**, so the office can sort requests by the day
  they are due to run.
- **"Please announce on" lists every air date.** A run of days is one request listing
  each date, not one request per day.
- **DETAILS repeats the facts even when the announcement text already states them.** The
  office asked for them; the office should not have to extract them from prose.
- **The contact is Mr. Pina** unless he names someone else. A student is never the
  contact.
- **A revision** replies on the original thread, with "REVISED" at the start of the
  subject and the full corrected announcement, never a list of changes.
- Mr. Pina sends every request from his school account. Student leads may draft; they do
  not send to the office.

## Writing the announcement

- **60 words or fewer.** About 20 seconds read aloud. The whole school hears it once and
  cannot reread it.
- **Order:** who, what, when, where, then one action. Nothing else.
- **Open with the name, not a hook:** "FRC Team 5669" in the first five words, so the
  listener knows who is talking before the details start.
- **One action, stated as an instruction:** "See Mr. Pina in the IDEA building." "Come to
  the shop after school." Never two asks in one announcement.
- **Written to be heard.** Every date as the date ("October 8"), time as "3:15 PM", room
  as it is said aloud. No URLs, no abbreviations a reader would stumble on, no parenthetical
  asides, no symbols. A link or QR code goes on the attached image or flyer, never in the
  text.
- **A weekday may accompany a date** ("Thursday, October 8"). The no-weekday rule in
  `IDEA_MATERIALS_PROCESS.md` exists because course materials are reused across years; an
  announcement airs once on a known date, so the reason does not apply.
- **Voice** per `Writing_Voice_Guide.md`, team-wide register: plain, direct, no pep talk,
  no exclamation points, no em dashes, American spelling. Real stakes stated plainly beat
  enthusiasm.
- **Room names are Mr. Pina's.** Never invent a room number or a room name for the IDEA
  building; take it from him or from IDEA Maps.

## FRC Team 5669

### Naming

- **"FRC Team 5669" is the fixed name** in every announcement. The team's name after the
  rebrand tied to coeducation is not settled, so the team name ("Techmen") is used only
  where Mr. Pina writes it himself.
- **"FIRST"** in text is all capitals, never plural or possessive. In an announcement read
  aloud the registered mark is not spoken; on an attached image it follows
  `FRC_Design_System.md` "FIRST usage rules".

### What the team announces

Each type has a fixed shape. Fill it; do not improvise a new structure.

| Type | Carries | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting or shop hours | days, time, place, who may come | FRC Team 5669 meets <when> in <place>. Any student may come. <action>. |
| Recruiting | who is wanted, what they will do, the one first step | FRC Team 5669 needs <builders, programmers, business, media>. <what they do, one clause>. <first step, date, place>. |
| Certification release | the certification's official name, who may take it, where to start | FRC Team 5669 is opening <official certification name>. Any student using the IDEA shop may certify. <where to start>. |
| Competition or event | event, date, place, how to watch or help | FRC Team 5669 competes at <event> on <date>. <how to follow or help>. |
| Result | event, the plain result, thanks where owed | FRC Team 5669 finished <result> at <event>. <one line of what it means or who made it possible>. |

- **Certification names are the official names** from the certification catalog, exactly
  as they appear in IDEA Classroom and the FRC app. Never paraphrase one.
- **A result states the result.** No softening a loss, no inflating a win. "Finished 12th
  of 40 and made the playoff alliance" is the pattern.
- **Individual students are not named** in an announcement unless Mr. Pina lists the
  names himself. The team is the subject.
- **Other IDEA announcements** (an IDEA event, an event the IDEA building hosts) follow
  the same rules, with "The IDEA pathway" or the event's own name in place of the team.

### The image

- One image, attached, when the announcement would benefit from one. Team logo or a real
  photo of the robot or the shop in use. Marks are used as supplied, per
  `FRC_Design_System.md`.
- A flyer only when it carries something the text cannot: a schedule grid, a sign-up QR
  code, a map. The office refuses a flyer that repeats the message.
- The image is a PNG or JPG. Text on it is large enough to read on a phone.

### Cadence

- **Plan backward from the air date.** Before the team's season, a recurring item such as
  shop hours airs once at the start of each quarter and again when it changes, not weekly.
- **An item with a deadline** (a sign-up, a certification window) airs twice: once when it
  opens and once two school days before it closes.

## What Claude does when asked for an announcement

1. Writes the announcement against "Writing the announcement" and counts its words.
2. Computes the submission deadline from the air date per rule 4 and states it in one line.
   Where that deadline has already passed, says so and gives the earliest air date still
   reachable.
3. Builds the request email in the shape above, with the exact To and Cc.
4. Creates it as a **Gmail draft** in Mr. Pina's account and does not send it. Sending is
   his, until he says otherwise.
5. Where the announcement needs a fact only Mr. Pina holds (a room, a time, who may
   attend), asks for that one fact rather than filling it in.

## Open

- **Whether announcements are read aloud, printed, or both** is not confirmed. The
  60-word, written-to-be-heard rules hold either way.
