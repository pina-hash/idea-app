# IDEA Morning Announcements Standard
**Version 1.6 - 2026-10-21**

How FRC Team 5669 and the IDEA pathway get an item into Bosco Tech's morning
announcements: where the request goes, when it is due, what the email looks like, how the
announcement itself is written, and what Claude does when asked for one. FRC is the main
user and has its own section. Voice rules live in `Writing_Voice_Guide.md`; this file
applies them to one channel.

**Sources.** Two kinds, and each rule is one or the other:

- **Office rules** come from Cathy Rivera's email "Morning Announcements" to all students,
  faculty-staff and coaches, 2026-09-30 10:43 AM, which restates a policy first set on
  2026-08-18.
- **House pattern** comes from Mr. Pina's own sent requests from `apina@boscotech.edu`,
  read 2026-09-30: Wed 8/13/2025, Wed 10/29/2025, Mon 11/03, Mon 11/10, Mon 11/17,
  Mon 12/01, Mon 12/08, Mon 12/15/2025, Fri 8/21/2026 and Mon 8/24/2026. Four were read in
  full (10/29, 11/03, 12/15/2025, 8/24/2026); the rest from their previews. Four carry an
  office reply confirming they ran ("Will do", "Done and ready to go", "Got it"), and none
  was refused. Where a rule below says "house
  pattern", it is what those emails actually do, not a new invention.

Where the office changes a rule, the office wins and this file is updated in the same turn.

## Submission (office rules)

1. **To:** Cathy Rivera, `crivera@boscotech.edu`.
   **Cc, every time:** Miguel Ramirez `mramirez@boscotech.edu` and Gabriella Holguin
   `gholguin@boscotech.edu`. On days Mrs. Rivera is out, Ms. Holguin prepares and sends
   the announcements. Requests before the 2026-27 year cc'd `scabrera@boscotech.edu`;
   that address is no longer on the list.
2. **Never send a request to the shared announcement email address.** The office said
   requests sent there can be missed.
3. **Due 2:00 PM the day before it airs.** A revision has the same deadline. Late requests
   have been accepted (12/15/2025 sent on a Saturday night, 8/21/2026 sent after 2:00 PM),
   but that is the office being generous, never something to plan on.
4. **Weekend rule.** A request received over the weekend airs Tuesday at the earliest.
   Derived from that: **to air on a Monday, the request is in by 2:00 PM the Friday
   before.** Check `calendar_2026-09-01_2027-03-01.pdf` for a non-school day in between.
5. **The request states the date, time, location and person to contact.** In the house
   pattern all four live inside the announcement text plus the Contact line; no separate
   details block is needed.
6. **No flyer unless it carries more than the message.** One colorful image instead. The
   house pattern already does this: a robot photo inline above the text.

## The request email (house pattern)

**Subject:** `Morning Announcement Request for <Ddd M/D>`, for example
`Morning Announcement Request for Mon 12/15`. One email per air date; each date gets its
own subject so the office can file it by day.

**Body, in this order:**

```
Hello,

I would like to request a morning announcement for <Ddd M/D>:

[robot image, inline, pasted into the body]

**FRC Team 5669 - Techmen Robotics** is holding <what> **TODAY - <Weekday>, <Month Dth>**
at **<time or "the start of lunch">** in the **<room>**.

<One or two sentences: who it is for and why they should come.>

See you there! Thank you, **FRC Team 5669 | Techmen**
Contact: Mr. Pina | apina@boscotech.edu

Thank you,
<Mr. Pina's school signature>
```

- **"Hello,"** opens it. Not "Good Morning" or "Hello Cathy" (both were used in 2025;
  "Hello," is the current form).
- **The image is inline in the body, above the announcement**, not an attachment below
  it. The robot photo (`IMG_58013.png`, the transparent robot render, or `frc2.png`) is
  the standing choice.
- **Formatting is part of the announcement.** The office pastes it into the bulletin as
  sent, so bold carries. Bold the team name and every fact a student must retain: what,
  the date, the time, the room, and "mandatory" when it is. Nothing else is bold.
- **Day-of and advance versions differ only in the time phrase.** Day-of: "TODAY -
  Monday, December 15th". Advance: "on Monday, August 24th". Everything else is
  identical, so the text reads right whichever day it runs.
- **A run of days is one email per day**, each with its own subject and its own time
  phrase.
- **Tech Announcements** is a second channel Mr. Pina has asked the office for (10/29/2025:
  "as well as Tech Announcements for each day leading up to the meeting"). Ask for it in
  the same email when a meeting needs a daily lead-up.
- A revision replies on the original thread with the full corrected announcement, never a
  list of changes.
- Mr. Pina sends every request. Student leads may draft; they do not send to the office.

## Writing the announcement

- **About 60 words for the announcement text**, not counting the sign-off and Contact
  lines. The requests read in full run 45 to 60.
- **Order:** team name, what, when, where, then who it is for. The house pattern opens
  with the team name every time.
- **One action.** "Come see what the team builds." "Stop by, meet the team." Never two
  asks.
- **Weekdays and full dates are used**: "TODAY - Monday, December 15th". The no-weekday
  rule in `IDEA_MATERIALS_PROCESS.md` is for course materials reused across years; an
  announcement airs once on a known date.
- **Time as the students know it.** "The start of lunch" when the bell schedule for that
  day puts lunch there; a clock time ("11:10 AM") only when it is not obvious. Check
  `IDEA_bell_schedules.md` for that day's schedule, because lunch moves.
- **Voice** per `Writing_Voice_Guide.md`: plain, direct, no em dashes, American spelling.
  "See you there!" and one emoji at the sign-off are the house pattern and stay; no other
  exclamation points.
- **Room names are Mr. Pina's.** "IDEA Classroom" is the standing meeting room. Never
  invent another.

## FRC Team 5669

### Naming (house pattern)

- **Header:** "FRC Team 5669 - Techmen Robotics", bold. **Sign-off:** "FRC Team 5669 |
  Techmen", bold. Use a hyphen where the 2025 emails used an en dash.
- If the team rebrands, both strings change together here first.
- **FIRST** in text is all capitals, never plural or possessive. An image carrying the
  FIRST mark follows `FRC_Design_System.md` "FIRST usage rules".

### What the team announces

Recorded types, each from a real accepted request, plus the new one coming:

| Type | Real example | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting, open to newcomers | 8/13/2025, 8/24/2026 | "first meeting of the year", open to all grade levels, no experience required |
| Mandatory member meeting that also recruits | 10/29, 11/03, 11/10, 11/17, 12/01, 12/08/2025 | **mandatory for all current Robotics Team members**, then one line inviting anyone interested |
| Last chance before a break | 12/15/2025 | "final mandatory meeting of the semester", "last chance to stop by, meet the team, and get information before the break" |
| Certification release | none yet | the certification's official name exactly as in the catalog, who may take it, where to start |

- **A mandatory meeting says "mandatory" in bold**, and names who it is mandatory for.
- **Certification names are the official names** from the certification catalog, as they
  appear in IDEA Classroom and the FRC app. Never paraphrase one.
- **Individual students are not named** unless Mr. Pina lists the names himself.
- **Other IDEA announcements** follow the same rules, with "The IDEA pathway" or the
  event's own name in place of the team.

### Cadence (house pattern)

- **Recurring meetings get one request per meeting**, sent days ahead. Fall 2025 ran a
  weekly Monday lunch meeting with a day-of announcement each week.
- **A first meeting gets two:** advance notice the school day before, day-of the morning
  of.

## FRC weekly rotation and automation (from 1.2)

Mr. Pina's decisions of 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-14:

- **Two days a week: Tuesday and Friday (from 2026-10-19).** Both are mandatory team
  meetings right after school in the IDEA Classroom: Tuesday plans the week, Friday
  recaps it and sets weekend plans, then the team builds until 5:00 PM (Friday also
  coaches kids' robotics teams 4:30 to 6:00 PM). Each announcement is stern with members
  and invites new students to come see what the team is about. Monday gets no
  announcement: the Mon 10/19 lunch meeting was the last one. Mon, Wed and Thu are open
  shop days, never called optional. Friday carries the Beach Blitz countdown while it is
  coming.
- **Every announcement serves a purpose and has one same-day action.**
- **The bulletin is also read over the PA.** Wording is optimized for being heard
  first and read second: team name first, short sentences, nothing hard to say
  aloud ("design", not "CAD"), the key fact repeated.
- **Each request carries a dated flyer PDF** (`Flyer_Techmen Robotics <Name>.pdf`, the
  office's own naming) with a specific date and time, a large QR code and a tapped
  link to the team app, and the robot photo with plain-language callouts on what
  students made. Solid colors only.
- **Written for someone who knows nothing about robots.** No team jargon in the
  spoken text or on a flyer: not FRC, FIRST, FLL or CAD. Say "the Techmen robotics
  team", "coach kids' robotics teams", "model every part in 3D", and name an event
  as "a robotics competition" the first time. What students made is described by
  what it took (designed, machined, wired, coded), never a word that undersells it
  such as "cut". Short beats complete: when a line needs explaining, cut the line.
- **Attendance is a written rule.** Members sign the Team 5669 handbook: 80% of
  required team meetings or removal, tracked by app check-in, modeled on Team 1678.
  The Tuesday announcement and every meeting flyer say "Attendance is taken" and the
  80% rule.
- **Flyers keep the poster grammar (2026-10-21).** All-caps hero, the ghost 5669 behind the robot, leader-line callouts, the gold join band and the hazard stripe are the look. Rounded gray cards and sentence-case headings read as cheap; Mr. Pina rejected a redesign built that way. Fix defects inside the existing look. Nothing on a flyer is under 9 pt, and Beach Blitz and every competition read "every member, every day" (handbook), never as optional.
- **Recruiting leans on peers.** Research (2026-10-14, "How Elite FRC Teams
  Recruit") found peer invitation the strongest recruiter, so Tuesday says "Team
  members, bring a friend."
- **Drafts, never sends.** A scheduled task at 7:50 AM each weekday runs
  `tools/frc-announcements/announce.py --auto`, leaves a Gmail draft for the next
  school day when it is a Tuesday or Friday, and notifies Mr. Pina with the
  flyer to attach. To change one day, he edits the draft before sending.

## What Claude does when asked for an announcement

1. Checks the bell schedule for the air date and the meeting date.
2. Computes the submission deadline per rule 4 and states it in one line. Where it has
   passed, says so and gives the earliest air date still reachable.
3. Writes the announcement in the house pattern above.
4. Creates each request as a **Gmail draft** in `apina@boscotech.edu` with the exact To,
   Cc and subject, and does not send it. The robot image is pasted in by Mr. Pina, since
   a draft cannot carry an inline image reliably. Sending is his until he says otherwise.
5. Where the announcement needs a fact only Mr. Pina holds (a room, a time, who may
   attend), asks for that one fact rather than filling it in.

## Open
